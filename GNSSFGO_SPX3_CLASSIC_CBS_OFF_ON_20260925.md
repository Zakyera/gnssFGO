The ON **GLIM-only graph** also degrades before any Kimera graph factor is added, so one cannot attribute the ON/OFF graph gap solely to the Kimera factors in gnssFGO. This observation does not identify a unique cause among changed source beliefs, source feedback, arrival schedule, graph reconstruction/correlation assumptions, or other source-run effects; no corrective mechanism was tried.
