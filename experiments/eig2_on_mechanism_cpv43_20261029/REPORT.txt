BOTTOM LINE
The non-covariance investigation narrows the problem to how Kimera's coupled
native/CBS graph responds to admitted relative constraints, rather than a
large bogus position arriving over the wire. It does NOT identify a specific
faulty equation yet. The first substantial ON tilt corrections and elevated
native IMU cost precede any marginalization or peer-factor retirement.
Later, the existing native objective audit records four accepted uphill
updates, one exceeding 5.5x its own pre-solve objective. The peer-edge residual
can improve while the overall graph objective worsens.

This supports prioritizing nonlinear solve/linearization, pose-velocity-bias/
gravity coupling, and full-objective acceptance. It does not prove that more
iterations alone fixes the run, nor exonerate later marginal-prior behavior.
Covariance, weights and all estimator settings were left untouched and were
not investigated. No new capture, optimizer solve, build or ATE/RPE scoring.
