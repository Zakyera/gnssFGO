4. Why the June force flags do not eliminate the later issue
-----------------------------------------------------------
W/src/gtsam/gtsam/nonlinear/ISAM2.cpp:733-742 adds marginal LinearContainerFactors
and marks their keys in fixedVariables_. ISAM2-impl.h:382-387 applies the
forceFullSolve threshold, then excludes those fixed keys from relinearization.
Therefore forcing an update/full solve is not a current-chart refresh of all
boundary factors. These are fixed LINEARIZATION anchors, not frozen estimated
robot poses. This was already understood in July; the code does not establish
a new GTSAM bug. Simply unfixing keys or resetting prior anchors would change
the represented prior and is not an authorized repair.

FL4p's localized old-chart IMU discrepancy and CP-K5's late IMU growth remain
relevant, but are not proof that one factor alone caused every CP-K5 failure.
CP-K1 shows a different problem when the global fallback is enabled. Keep the
two mechanisms separate rather than calling both "temporary-factor removal".
