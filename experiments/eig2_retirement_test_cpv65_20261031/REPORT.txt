same initial states, caches, noise, priors and all unaffected factors:
 A actual: old factor removed, new peer factor included.
 B removal control: old retained for this solve, new included.
 C old retained, new omitted.
 D old removed, new omitted.
A-B isolates immediate retirement with incoming information held fixed; B-C
isolates admission with old information held fixed; the four-arm interaction
exposes nonadditivity. No comparing removal-only extra steps to ordinary fused
updates. Every arm uses exactly one native ordinary step plus one refresh.

A must reproduce actual native equations and states before counterfactuals are
admitted. All targets must be exact native factor identities. Preserved priors
keep original anchors/functions, smart factors stay native. Retaining an old
factor is legal only before its endpoint is marginalized; no dangling keys and
no persistent policy promotion. B/C end before marginalization. Each event
starts independently from the original baseline, not a modified prior event.
Measure actual output/revision vectors, window shape, bias/velocity and native
fit; do not assume improved residual fit or less revision means more accuracy.
