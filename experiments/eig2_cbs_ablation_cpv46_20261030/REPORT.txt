CP-V46 -- EIG-2 saved-checkpoint CBS equation ablation
2026-10-30. COMPLETE: Jacobian correction does NOT fix the early tilt.

Decision
--------
Do not launch a full sequence or promote the exact-Jacobian candidate on the
basis of this test. The confirmed approximation is not the main explanation
of the large ordinary-step window rotations in these five checkpoints.
Removing current explicit CBS factors actually increases tilt magnitude in
all five windows. This rejects a simple "the current incoming constraint alone
is forcing the large tilt" account; it does not exonerate ON history or show
that CBS is healthy over a whole mission.

What was tested
---------------
Five independent CP-V44 after_ordinary snapshots, updates21--25. Start is
each snapshot's initial Values, with the full newly assembled graph. Native
smart measurements, IMU PIM, initial priors, bias factors, weights, ordering
and elimination stay unchanged. One GN step per arm, no iterative search.

A: remove explicit CBS Pose3 Between equations (17/18/19/20/21 factors).
B: original complete graph/equations; first reproduce captured ordinary solve.
C: B with only CBS Local/Log chain-rule Jacobians corrected. Identical residual
and whitening, same Gaussian slot/key order. Non-CBS Gaussian factors are the
exact same objects from the native linearization; no native equation changed.
No global macro, production library, default or source configuration modified.

A is NOT a fresh CBS-OFF run. Its initial states and visual measurements were
produced under ON. No marginal priors/fixed keys exist here, so no explicit CBS
is hidden in marginal priors; earlier ON influence in state/history remains.
All three share the same starting values; arms/windows do not feed each other.
