case directory: Python3.10 in Humble has no hashlib.file_digest. start.json
and failed.txt preserve this harness failure. The original executable was
never invoked, no source/controller replay needs repeating.

New run_compat.py imports the unchanged run.py and replaces ONLY its hashing
helper with streaming SHA256 supported by Python3.10. Identical SHA256
definition, no bypassed hash assertion. Its save wrapper uses start_compat.json
instead of overwriting start.json. Original run.py / input_pins remain frozen;
compatibility_pins.json records the new entry point and original manifests.
All launch commands, model settings, source subsequences and validation remain
unchanged. Refuse any existing receiver launch receipt. This is a prelaunch
infrastructure repair, not a retry of a numerical/estimator experiment.
