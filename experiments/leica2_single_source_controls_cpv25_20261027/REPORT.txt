1. Decision

The proposed controls isolate TWO effects, not one:
(a) Adding the less accurate OFF GLIM belief stream worsens the Kimera-only
receiver's common-mask position RMSE from1.323540m to2.006010m (+51.56%).
The BOTH receiver fails to retain the healthier source's accuracy here.
(b) Even WITHOUT competing sources, the receiver adds newest-output instability:
ONLINE1s rotation RPE exceeds each corresponding native source. Thus inter-source
conflict alone does NOT explain the jitter. Finalized single-source outputs
greatly improve local motion while retaining roughly the same global ATE.

Native Kimera to Kimera-only receiver ATE also worsens1.094234->1.323540m
(+20.96%); GLIM native to GLIM-only changes2.782169->2.807562m (+0.91%).
These are controlled observations, not a unique proof of covariance calibration,
correlation, frame or numerical bugs. The source-to-receiver representation,
revisions, reconstruction model and publication semantics differ from native
outputs. Do not attribute the20.96% to one of those without another test.

8. What to investigate next — proposed, not executed

Do not immediately rerun sources or select another Qc multiplier. First
isolate the receiver's newest-state prediction using this cleaner Kimera-only
case: compare its same-state pose/angular velocity immediately around a new
Kimera update and support prediction with the later corrected state. Existing
mixed-source traces localized this mechanism, but the new control demonstrates
it persists WITHOUT a competing source. Use a bounded saved-journal trace,
not another full sensor campaign; no code change or replay is authorized here.

Separately, BOTH-vs-Kimera-only establishes a mixed-source accuracy penalty in
this OFF case. A later health/covariance treatment should be judged against
the Kimera-only reference, not merely against the inferior original BOTH
score. These controls do not determine whether a scalar covariance multiplier,
directional health selection, cross-correlation handling or another model change
is the right repair. Do not conflate this with native source-CBS effects.
