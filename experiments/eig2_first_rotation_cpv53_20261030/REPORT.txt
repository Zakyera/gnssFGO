Both saved effective source configurations use /boxi/cpt7/imu and cpt7_imu
as body; Kimera saved PIM has identity body-to-sensor rotation. The GLIM message
header is 'map', the configured world label; child/body is cpt7_imu. Kimera's
configured external-pose and base frames both equal cpt7_imu. Header 'map' alone
does not mean a relative motion is expressed in a different physical body.
Different world orientations cancel in a relative transform T_from^-1*T_to.

RECOMMENDED NEXT DECISION -- NOT LAUNCHED
Before changing weights or inserting a corrective rotation, inspect GLIM's own
state at this exact early edge: contemporaneous gyro bias, native IMU deltaR,
bias-corrected prediction, and IMU-vs-LiDAR rotation residuals before/after its
update. This distinguishes a legitimate bias/scan-matching compromise from an
internal GLIM support/frame/linearization error. The saved data here lack that
complete instantaneous GLIM state. If further capture is approved, use only a
short unchanged-settings passive observation prefix, not another full campaign.
Do not substitute final map biases or call raw gyro independent truth. Nothing
in this result justifies a new tuning change or a blanket 'CBS is fixed' claim.
