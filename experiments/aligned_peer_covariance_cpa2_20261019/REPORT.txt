6. Mathematical covariance contract
-----------------------------------
Let a source right-local uncertainty be Z'=Z Exp(delta_z), and the mean map be
 A=L Z R. With L and R treated as exact constants:
 A'=L Z Exp(delta_z) R = A Exp(Ad_(R^-1) delta_z).
Therefore even the deterministic transport should in general be:
 Sigma_A = J_Z Sigma_Z J_Z^T, J_Z=Ad_(R^-1).
Simply copying Sigma_Z is exact only in special invariant/identity cases. Left
multiplication cancels for right-local source perturbations; a large change of
the mean alone cannot quantify the necessary J_Z correction.

If L and R also have right-local perturbations, the first-order Jacobians are:
 J_L=Ad_((Z R)^-1), J_Z=Ad_(R^-1), J_R=I.
For the joint18D covariance of [delta_L,delta_Z,delta_R]:
 Sigma_A = [J_L J_Z J_R] Sigma_joint [J_L J_Z J_R]^T.
Only if the corresponding cross terms are explicitly assumed zero does this
reduce to the familiar sum of three propagated marginal contributions.

Here the bridges use receiver states/biases and shared native IMU. Independence
is not established: left/right/source errors can be correlated. Adding arbitrary
bridge variances while dropping all cross terms is not automatically exact or
conservative. Transport can change covariance orientation/cross terms without
uniformly inflating its trace. This is not a justification for scalar scaling.

The current path performs neither J_Z transport nor a declared propagated
bridge covariance. It is a deliberate mean-only approximation, not the old
Stage4 aligned-covariance machinery from the separate EKF experiments.
All-LOCAL provenance and complete temporal covariance propagation are different
requirements; passing the former does not establish the latter.
