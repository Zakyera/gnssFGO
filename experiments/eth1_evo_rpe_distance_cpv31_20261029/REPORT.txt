WHAT IS BEING COMPARED
----------------------
Method1: saved CP-V27 GLIM first-published absolute MAIN pose, Kimera recovered
absolute backend pose at the same output update, fusion original ONLINE newest
support. Kimera method1 is NOT its native incremental ROS odometry. Original
six-significant-digit Kimera backend logging rounding bound <=0.504mm remains.
Historical absolute points are not retroactively replaced with refined poses.

Method2: saved CP-V28 GLIM/fusion accumulated relative motions from two states
in the SAME publication solution; Kimera original native incremental ROS.
Both plotting and evaluation use that accumulated trajectory. This is not
finalized fixed-lag output and not the authors' universal benchmark policy.
Use original historical OFF/ON together, not fresh CP-V29 OFF plus old ON.

three estimators under both policies. In chosen method2, ON translation RPE

attribute its1.802/0.889m RPE to the native incremental ROS output.

not establish global ATE improvement, calibrated covariance or a causal fault.
