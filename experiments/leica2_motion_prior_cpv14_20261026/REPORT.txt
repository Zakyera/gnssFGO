CP-V14 — LEICA-2 VELODYNE, ONE STRONGER WNOA MOTION-PRIOR TEST
2026-10-26. COMPLETED. Operational PASS; strict scientific screen FAIL.
Decision: useful partial jitter reduction, NOT a complete fix or a promoted default.

1. Answer in plain language

We made the fusion's motion constraint twice as strong, without changing what
GLIM or Kimera sent. The newest output became less jumpy in both CBS modes.
This is a real estimator change, not trajectory smoothing after the run.
The reduction also occurs while moving and turning, not only while stationary.

However, the excessive ONLINE path is still present, and global position RMSE
did not improve. It increased by 3.182mm OFF and 0.634mm ON (0.159%/0.035%).
Those are small changes relative to roughly2m ATE, not evidence of a meaningful
catastrophic regression; they nevertheless fail the predeclared no-worsening
criterion. We have not changed that criterion after seeing the results.

Interpretation: the ongoing motion-versus-source balance contributes to the
newest-state jitter. This does NOT establish that the initial pose anchor or
historical marginal prior is too weak. It does not establish an optimal Qc,
calibrated source uncertainty, or a cross-sequence improvement. The larger-scale
trajectory error remains largely unaffected. Keep the original configuration.

2. Exactly what was changed

One predeclared scalar: Qc_new=0.5*Qc_old in the private receiver Qc() helper.
Old diag: [.00375,.00375,.00375,.015,.015,.015].
New diag: [.001875,.001875,.001875,.0075,.0075,.0075].
These are process-noise covariance parameters, NOT standard deviations or
GLIM/Kimera published covariance. At a fixed motion residual the information
and quadratic cost double; corresponding standard deviations divide by sqrt2.
The initial priors were unchanged. Existing marginal priors were not manually
rescaled; new marginals develop naturally under the changed motion model.

One full saved original CP-V4 OFF journal, then one ON journal, each once.
No source/bag reruns, extra candidate weights, sweep, numerical retries or
default changes. Private receiver differs from the exactly reproduced CP-V13
observer build by ONE line. Live.cpp and Trace.h are byte-identical to CP-V13.
Seven-second lag, independent100ms supports, source_wnoa, ISAM2 policy,
no central IMU/GNSS, source means/full dense covariances/Gaussian scale1,
event order, stable-ID replacement/seal_at_absorption and EOF policy unchanged.
The imported source streams still contain their original CBS-OFF/ON settings;
we did not rerun or retune either source optimizer.

Qc also enters GP interpolation. The pre-replay100-case audit explicitly
verified scalar invariance of interpolated means/source residuals/Jacobians
and source noise, as well as actual discrete Q scaling and doubled motion
factor cost. Maximum differences: interpolated pose4.48e-16,
source residual4.72e-16, Jacobian3.96e-11, doubled-cost relative error1.27e-15.
This is a controlled change in mathematics, not a claim of unchanged dynamics.

9. Limitations and recommendation

Reference is shared-CPT7 IE-TC, NOT independent prism ground truth. Original
mean-only covariance alignment approximation is unchanged. Replaying saved
arrival order holds timing input fixed but does not measure live scheduling
effects or drone control stability. No nonlinear stationarity or marginal-prior
conditioning certificate. One sequence and one perturbation, no generalization
or statistical confidence claim. Tiny ATE changes should not be overstated.

Recommendation: retain this as evidence that the motion/source balance is a
useful lever for jitter, not as the final setting. Before any larger Qc sweep,
separate residual short-time source disagreement from global drift using these
saved traces; neither a weak initial anchor nor blind covariance inflation is
justified by this result. Any further experiment requires a new decision.
