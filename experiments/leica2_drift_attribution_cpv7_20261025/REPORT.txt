INTERPRETATION AND STOPPING DECISION
The evidence is consistent with weakening a useful heading constraint while
reducing harmful tilt influence. It explains why total short-interval rotation
RMS alone was an inadequate design target. It does NOT prove a simple
tilt-only multiplier will solve the problem, or that all original ON drift
comes from GLIM covariance: original ON already has worse heading than OFF.
Bidirectional feedback and changed state/mean histories matter. One realized
candidate capture is not repeated-trial causal/statistical certification.

Do not blanket discard GLIM->Kimera or choose another global rotation scale
from this result. The next DESIGN question is direction-aware trust (tilt vs
heading), with long-horizon/global safeguards. Any implementable direction
must come from online-estimated gravity/state, never evaluation ground truth.
No coefficient, gate, one-way run or directional candidate is approved here.
This bounded audit is complete; stop, retain the baseline, discuss next choice.
