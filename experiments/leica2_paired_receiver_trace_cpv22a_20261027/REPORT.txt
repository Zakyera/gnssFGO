revised state. Those corrections are much larger with Velodyne, including
corrections from the SAME Kimera beliefs. Older active states move far less.

centimetre-scale solver correction. Immediate marginalization effects are tiny.

Thus, the evidence favors excessive newest-state sensitivity to short-motion
evidence and the current motion/source information balance. It does NOT prove
a particular covariance multiplier, physical sensor defect, or all-code absence

large instantaneous marginalization jump. No source run, timestamp shift,

Let X[i|k] denote pose i in the graph at publication k. Fusion currently saves
A[k]=X[k|k]. Kimera-style output instead maintains
  O[k] = O[k-1] * inverse(X[k-1|k]) * X[k|k].
The previous pose on the right is REVISED, not the one published last cycle.
If one simply chains inverse(A[k-1])*A[k], it telescopes back to A[k] and cannot
remove the zigzag. The saved-data control verifies this to <=9.8e-13m.

An old published point stays fixed in history, while the graph can revise that
same point before producing the next one. Thus the apparent connected ONLINE
step contains BOTH motion over the latest interval AND correction of the old
point. In the fixed display frame the exact decomposition closes to <=2e-15m:
  A[k]-A[k-1] = (X[k|k]-X[k-1|k]) + (X[k-1|k]-X[k-1|k-1]).
Here the subtraction denotes transformed prism-point positions, not SE(3)
matrix subtraction. The p95 old-point correction is3.108cm Hesai/6.852cm Velo.

persistent path. It does NOT remove every physical/local-motion error.
