1. Result

The single authorized first-60-second ETH1 CBS-ON capture completed with native
GLIM, native Kimera and the frozen WNOA receiver co-running at 0.10x. Source,
covariance, lifecycle, conservation, causal insertion, publication and saved-
journal replay checks passed. No source retry, tuning or gate relaxation.

On 591 common physical scan stamps against the retained baseline's first 60 s,
the targeted LOCAL covariance rebuild median fell 162.828 -> 13.392 ms (about 12.2x).
However, total scan median fell only 513.142 -> 462.594 ms and p95 increased
791.005 -> 853.998 ms. The predeclared desirable p95 < 800 ms margin was NOT met.
The mandatory receiver p95-age < 1 s and queue < 1000 gates DID pass.

Thus E1 removes a real local cost but does not establish that all runtime delays
are solved. Main smoother, sidecar update and receiver refinement took longer
in this capture than in the historical prefix. Their cause is not established
by this comparison: scheduling, nonlinear history/workload and host conditions
are not controlled identical inputs. Do not label those increases a proven
regression caused by batching, or hide them behind the local speedup.

7. Decision and stop

E1 batching is numerically validated and its local speedup is reproduced live.
This 60 s operating point completed correctly, but the extra 20% scan-headroom
target failed and end-to-end tail improvement was NOT demonstrated. Keep the
candidate opt-in; do not change production defaults or certify 160 s/full ETH1.
The remaining source main/sidecar and receiver refinement costs are visible,
but their next optimization must remain a separate hypothesis/task. No E1b,
E2, new sequence, longer retry, tuning or additional experiment started here.
