CP-K2 — conditional stereo landmark fallback: NOT READY TO INTEGRATE
2026-10-19; saved data only, no estimator/source/bag replay.

Outcome
-------
The proposed conditional-landmark implementation fixes the derivative mismatch
for most retained visual factors, including the dominant offending factor.
It fails the predeclared all-factor gate. It is NOT an integrated or certified
repair of Kimera's update531 failure. The production build remains untouched.

What was implemented
--------------------
Standalone ConditionalStereo.h minimizes the same existing whitened stereo
residual in one 3D landmark at fixed camera poses. It reuses GTSAM's residual,
body extrinsics, missing-right handling, whitening and Schur construction.
No covariance/noise/measurement changes. At a stationary landmark, E^T b=0,
so the held-point and Schur gradients agree (envelope theorem locally).
This is valid locally only where a suitable finite conditional solution exists;
it is not a guarantee that every cached triangulation has such a solution.

The existing smart-factor activity set was held fixed only for this diagnostic:
173 valid factors attempted, 514 captured invalid/zero factors not activated.
Invalid factors were not silently removed from an estimator to lower its cost.
Nothing was linked into a production or candidate Kimera estimator.

Failures and why the candidate was stopped
-----------------------------------------
Two valid captured factors,106675 and108819, failed the bounded stationarity
gate. No iteration cap, covariance, measurement, or outer acceptance guard was
relaxed to hide them. In particular106675 has three left observations and ZERO
valid right observations despite being stored as a smart-stereo factor.

For106675 the retained point is8.1896m deep /8.3960m from the first camera.
During conditional minimization its point moved outward to a world-coordinate
norm of order1.7million metres while the cost decreased. Thus the problem is
not simply needing one more small finite-landmark correction. The code did not
accept this as stationary: scaled gradient1.6964e-9 exceeded1.2860e-10.

An independent read-only inverse-depth profile using the same measurements,
sigma3 and camera geometry confirms decreasing cost along a far-depth branch:

The diagnostic reproduces the saved initial cost within4.2e-12. It is evidence
of this escaping branch, NOT a proof of the global optimum or of the true
landmark's physical distance. No infinity factor was added to the estimator.
The original profile's landmarkDistanceThreshold=10 remains untouched; this
unconstrained diagnostic must not be installed as if it preserved that policy.

Separately, native Schur normal-equation elimination at factor107229 differed
from an independent QR projector reference by4.72545e-8 relative (predeclared

the original stop. No replacement covariance mathematics was installed.

Development and validation record
---------------------------------
All attempts are retained under the new run directory with distinct names.
Initial normal-equation/very-tight cost-tie handling hit a numerical plateau.
Direct QR for the same damped3D least-squares step and gradient-decreasing ties
within the predeclared1e-12 cost tolerance resolved that numerical issue, but
did NOT resolve the two escaping-depth factors. The final all-factor test exits
1 and records passed:false. No failure was retried as a source experiment.

Synthetic Release tests pass: nonidentity body extrinsics, missing-right rows,
dense Schur reference, deterministic repeat, input/cache nonmutation, negative
depth and singular-point rejection. Focused ASan/UBSan fixture status and final
historical-file/container checks are in final_checks.json. Linked libraries are
not sanitizer-instrumented; leak detection is disabled. No general production
transaction, covariance, source replay or whole-update success is claimed.

Decision and next engineering step
----------------------------------
Reject this conditional-minimization candidate for integration. Do not rerun
full ETH1, change gates, drop factors, cap their depth, or introduce infinity
handling as an unannounced workaround.

The next bounded design is a fallback that optimizes pose increments AND the
temporary 3D landmark increments jointly, checks the same joint stereo cost,
and consistently eliminates the landmark increments from the damped system.
That avoids requiring each landmark to reach a finite optimum with all poses
held fixed. It is a candidate direction, not a demonstrated cure. It must first
prove derivative/step/cost consistency on saved factors, and later commit/cache
and transaction parity, before any source prefix. No permanent landmark prior,
covariance scale change or method promotion follows from this report.
