BOTTOM LINE
The ON penalty is mostly a sustained trajectory-shape/drift discrepancy, not
one catastrophic ON spike. It persists in seven-second-finalized output.
The most useful investigation regions are50–70s and275–310s;170–178s is another
large accumulated-error region. Kimera's ON means are not uniformly better at
the relative-belief level, despite the major improvement in its full trajectory.
Matched, mature belief covariances also tighten more than the pooled median
suggested. Both observations support a weighting/correlation concern, but this
read-only analysis does not identify an exclusive causal mechanism.

WHAT IS ESTABLISHED / WHAT IS NOT
Established: location and magnitude of discrepancies; persistence after seven-
second finalization; local ON Kimera mean degradation in some regions; matched
late covariance tightening without commensurate reference residual improvement.
Not established: which persistent prior, cross-correlation, linearization,
orientation/translation coupling or model assumption contributes how much to ATE.
IE-TC sharesCPT7 and is not independent prismGT. Both sources show normalized
reference discrepancies incompatible with taking these matrices as a certified
uncertainty model; that comparison alone is not a calibration test. Incremental
parity is not nonlinear stationarity or health certification.

RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP — PROPOSAL ONLY
Use these exact saved windows for a parity-first receiver-only controlled test:
separate effect of revised Kimera means from its covariance schedule while
holding all timestamps/order/IDs/GLIM/supports fixed. Any replay/intervention
requires separate user approval. Do not tune a global covariance multiplier to
ATE or launch another source run based on this diagnosis. A graph-provenance
analysis is still needed to attribute tightening specifically to feedback.
