OUTCOME
One private bounded Kimera-only frozen-input replay was attempted. The new
safeguard shortened three harmful full steps, but stopped at update108 because

The transaction rolled back and the controller stopped playback. There was no

trajectory repair. No ATE/RPE conclusion is drawn from its stopped prefix.

WHY THIS CHANGE
FL4n established that the baseline cached and freshly evaluated same-theta
linear systems matched, and the Bayes-tree solve satisfied those equations,
yet the accepted nonlinear step could substantially increase the full cost.
FL4o tests one narrower hypothesis: keep the native GN direction, but shorten
the update when its nonlinear cost increases. It is not another covariance or
belief-policy experiment, and does not change the temporary-CBS/no-inheritance
semantics.

IMPLEMENTATION
- Overlay only, in a new private run directory. Production source/library
  files are not edited. The receiver/fuser is not run or changed.
- At each smoother update, retain the previous estimate plus initialized new
  variables. Perform the normal factor additions/removals and iSAM update.
  Before marginalization, compare both estimates on this SAME post-removal
  graph and its current owning theta chart.
- Evaluate normal nonlinear factors on fresh clones. For an unanchored
  LinearContainer marginal prior evaluate its saved Gaussian at
  Local(theta,X), including the constant; its ordinary error(X)==0 is not used.
- Full-step acceptance tolerance:1e-10*(1+abs(cost_before)). If the full step
  fails this test, interpolate the two deltas in theta coordinates with
  alpha=1/2,1/4,...,2^-16. Accept the first decrease exceeding that tolerance.
  Invalid cost or exhausted search throws. No LM fallback is included.
- Commit the accepted delta while retaining theta, undamped Bayes tree,
  cached Gaussian equations and factor covariances. Clear the pending delta
  recomputation mask and invalidate the covariance cache. This separates the
  accepted nonlinear step from the Gaussian minimizer, as step-controlled
  optimizers do. It is NOT a stationarity claim, covariance recalibration, or
  proof that covariance is newly linearized at the accepted point.
- Wrap the complete smoother update in a transaction. Restore tree, values,
  timestamps, active-CBS bookkeeping and slots on failure. SmartStereo object
  identities must survive because Kimera tracks these exact shared pointers.
  Their three mutable caches (result_, cameraPosesTriangulation_, Fs) are
  restored in place through a private nonvirtual header helper. Immutable
  measurements, calibration, parameters and pointers are not replaced.
- The candidate is scoped to the pinned Gaussian/active_window_temporary
  configuration, with health weighting and temporary-linear mode disabled.
  It is not a generic rollback guarantee for other BPSAM modes. The existing
  calculateBestEstimate() forces a raw GN solve; no such caller exists in the
  current Kimera path, and this candidate does not certify that API.

INTERPRETATION AND STOP
Step-length control can prevent some harmful accepted steps, but shrinking this
native proposal is insufficient to complete even the bounded replay. Sixteen
sampled failures do not prove that every conceivable step length is uphill,
identify a unique cause, or prove the beliefs are wrong. They do rule out
claiming that this particular bounded backtracking safeguard is a complete fix.

The next decision is about producing a reliable descent direction for the
original-chart objective (for example a chart-consistent damped local solve),
not relaxing the acceptance tolerance, adding covariance scaling, inheriting
external factors in the marginal prior, or simply running a longer sequence.
No such follow-up is implemented here. Any later candidate must retain the
current original-prior chart and no-inheritance constraints, and be validated
before promotion. Do not conclude CBS performance from this incomplete capture.
