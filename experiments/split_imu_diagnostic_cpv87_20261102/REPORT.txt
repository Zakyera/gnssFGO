BOTTOM LINE
The target GLIM/HG4930 + Kimera/Alphasense assignment is technically routed
correctly, but the Alphasense Kimera integration is not qualified for a campaign.
The healthy CPT7 control localizes the issue to the changed IMU integration
bundle or its interaction with the backend. It does not prove defective hardware.
The leading TESTABLE configuration hypothesis is the much tighter Alphasense
bias-evolution model interacting with real acceleration/model disagreement.
This is not a confirmed cause. No estimator, optimizer, replay, build, tuning,
calibration update, fusion, CBS-ON, deletion or new trajectory scoring was run.

7. DECISION / NEXT SINGLE EXPERIMENT (PROPOSED, NOT LAUNCHED)
Do not proceed to full split-IMU/CBS runs yet. The next discriminating intervention
is ONE matched60s Alpha CBS-OFF bias-random-walk-only ablation: retain Alpha raw
data,200Hz,white-noise densities,extrinsics,timing,initialization,priors and
backend; change ONLY its two bias random walks to the already-used CPT7 control
values(.0043 accel,.000266 gyro). Do not also change alignment or clipping.
This tests sensitivity to bias evolution; it is NOT an approved production
calibration or a claim that CPT7 noise describes Alpha hardware.
Retain physical bias/velocity and separate inertial/bias/prior error diagnostics
if an observational capture can be added without changing estimator behavior.
Compare the same EVO mask, trajectory revisions and onset. A healthy result
would support a model-configuration interaction, not prove sensor independence
or final uncertainty calibration. Failure would keep initialization/timing/
calibration and numerical interaction open; do not start a parameter sweep.

Current logging lacks actual bias histories, individual unwhitened IMU residuals,
preintegrated deltas/Jacobians and factor-by-factor bias errors. These cannot be
recovered uniquely from saved poses. They are the main causal-diagnosis limit.
