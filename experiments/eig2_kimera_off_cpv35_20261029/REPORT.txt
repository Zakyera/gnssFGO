Interpretation and next decision
--------------------------------
Confirmed: this OFF baseline's shaking is reproducible without another estimator;
large absolute-history revisions coexist with real internal window inconsistency.
Not supported as sole cause: Velodyne configuration, incoming GLIM frame/timing,
or receiver WNOA. Original Hesai/Velodyne Kimera OFF are numerically identical.
Not fully excluded: native camera/IMU calibration, initialization/bias handling,
visual degeneracy, or a defect in our modified BPSAM/fixed-lag integration.
This is NOT a verdict on an untouched upstream Kimera implementation.

Leading hypothesis: large one-step incremental Gauss-Newton corrections are
being computed using unsuitable/stale linearization and retained-prior charts.
Large anchor departures and nonlinearly bad IMU residuals are consistent with
that mechanism, but do not alone distinguish linearization error, cached-factor
inconsistency, inadequate solve policy, or an upstream input/model trigger.
The measurement/model weights were unchanged. No convergence/stationarity
claim is justified by a solver return, smaller incremental error or this audit.

Recommended next controlled experiment, NOT launched/implemented here:
1. Capture a faithful full nonlinear checkpoint around onset and1335--1338,
   including visual observations, IMU preintegration/bias parameters, cached
   Gaussian factors and marginal prior with its owning chart.
2. In an isolated copied graph with SAME measurements/weights and unchanged
   original-chart marginal prior, compare the incremental step against a
   carefully validated nonlinear solve with step/cost/gradient diagnostics.
   Confirm cloned smart-factor objective/linearization semantics first; the
   historic CP-K1 error-versus-Schur mismatch forbids treating arbitrary LM
   error reduction as proof. Never unlock/re-anchor a marginalized prior naively.
3. If the copy recovers small IMU residuals, trace cached vs freshly linearized
   contributions and prior chart handling; only then choose one targeted fix.
   If not, investigate input/model conflict or state observability first.
4. Revalidate OFF before investigating added CBS-ON effects. Do not compensate
   with WNOA tuning, smoothing or altered trajectory-output semantics.
