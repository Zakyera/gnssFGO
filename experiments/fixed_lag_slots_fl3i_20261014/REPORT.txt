FL3i — bounded marginal-factor slot storage
Date: 2026-10-14
Status: SLOT_GROWTH_FIXED; FULL_NUMERICAL_GATE_FAILED; NOT_PROMOTED

Outcome
-------
The isolated implementation removes the observed empty-slot growth. The one
complete 148.58 s recorded replay keeps at most 175 factor slots rather than
FL3h's 1,571, with the same source-version accounting and source payloads.
However, it fails the numerical-equivalence limits declared before running.
The largest as-played position difference from FL3h is 0.279428 mm; RMS is
0.018426 mm. Small does not mean the declared gate passed. Production and the
previous validated FL3h implementation are unchanged. No second full replay,
tolerance relaxation, source run, live run, or automatic promotion occurred.

Root cause and isolated change
------------------------------
Pinned runtime: GTSAM 4.2a8, from
/home/yeranis/results/gnssfgo_synthetic_uncertainty_20261004/baseline/runtime
Pinned implementation:
/home/yeranis/workspaces/gnssfgo_ct_runtime/rwth_gtsam_src/gtsam/nonlinear/ISAM2.cpp
Its ISAM2::marginalizeLeaves(), lines 659–686, appends marginal factors before
removing summarized factors. Unlike ordinary insertion, it does not honor
findUnusedFactorSlots for those marginal insertions. Active factors and states
remain bounded while the backing array acquires more empty slots.

Private SlotISAM2::marginalizeLeaves() copies the pinned elimination/Bayes-tree
surgery verbatim. Only final bookkeeping changes, SlotISAM2.cpp:209–242:
remove summarized factors and their index entries first; add the identical
Gaussian marginals/LinearContainerFactors through add_factors with the existing
reuse flag; update linear cache and VariableIndex at the returned indices.
No surviving factor is compacted or remapped. Source ownership uses actual
returned slots. Fixed linearization points, fixed keys and marginal information
are unchanged. No graph reload, new Schur calculation, extra solve or optimizer
policy change is introduced. Installed libraries are not edited/relinked.
