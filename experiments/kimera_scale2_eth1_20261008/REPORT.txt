ETH1: receiver-only Kimera covariance x2 sensitivity
2026-10-08

Status: COMPLETE. Primary runs, evaluation and exact deterministic repeats passed.
This is an isolated experiment, not a change to the production configuration.

Result
======
Downweighting Kimera improves fused ATE in both CBS modes on this window.
It does not eliminate the CBS-ON versus CBS-OFF loss. Final 1-second translation
RPE worsens in both modes. Thus it is a mixed sensitivity result, not evidence
that a universal Kimera covariance multiplier has been calibrated.

Scope and implementation
========================
Exactly two new primary receiver runs, CBS-OFF and CBS-ON, using the retained
matched ETH1 event streams. Neither native estimator was rerun. The original
GLIM/Kimera exchange remained classic CBS, Gaussian, with its original settings.
No estimator executable, source code, launch profile, default, or raw log changed.

Only Kimera's FULL covariance was multiplied by 2 at the downstream receiver
input, for ALL 14,250 Kimera versions per mode. All 36 entries, including cross
terms, were scaled. Every relative mean, physical endpoint, availability time,
event position, source/session/BeliefID and revision number was retained.
Every GLIM/clock/end record is byte-identical to the original journal.

Sigma_K_used = 2 Sigma_K_reported; information_K_used = 0.5 information_K_reported.
Whitened residual/Jacobian scale is 1/sqrt(2) at a fixed state. The resulting
nonlinear trajectory response need not be halved. This is uniform Gaussian
weighting, not Huber, residual gating, additive model noise or health weighting.

The transform produced new *.events journals. It did not overwrite source CSVs.
The unchanged receiver calls its input covariance 'reported' in its output CSV:
in these derived runs that column therefore contains the already scaled input,
NOT what native Kimera originally reported. Use the raw journals and manifest
to distinguish original source covariance from experimental effective covariance.

Frozen model
============
source_wnoa, full-history iSAM2, no central IMU factors, no bias/acceleration states.
State: Pose3, world velocity, body angular velocity. Independent 100 ms support
clock; 59-sample timestamp-only startup. WNOA Qc [.00375,.00375,.00375,.015,.015,.015].
Initial pose sigma 1e-5, velocity sigma 10 m/s, angular-rate sigma 3 rad/s.
Full 6D source factors; stable-ID replacement. Strict nominal publication
stationarity <=1e-5. GLIM covariance x1. No additional receiver model noise.
The source estimators still use their original IMUs. Removing central IMU
factors is not a claim that all source information is statistically independent.

Interpretation
==============
1. Kimera's weighting materially affects global fitted trajectory shape here.
   Halving its information improves final ATE from 4.53 to 3.69 cm OFF (-18.61%)
   and 11.69 to 8.46 cm ON (-27.60%). Causal as-played ATE improves too,
   OFF -16.92% and ON -24.32%, not just retrospective.
2. This is not a uniform accuracy improvement: final translation RPE worsens
   from 16.43 to 17.74 mm OFF (+8.00%) and 27.22 to 28.44 mm ON (+4.46%). Final rotation RPE
   worsens modestly OFF and is effectively unchanged ON.
3. At the SAME x2 setting, ON remains worse than OFF in ATE and both RPEs.
   Do not compare modified ON only against unmodified OFF and call that matched.
4. This does not prove Kimera's relative means are generally wrong, that its
   uncertainty is calibrated by x2, or that source dependence has been corrected.
   ATE and short relative error measure different properties. One interval is
   insufficient to choose a universal multiplier or a new production baseline.
5. No x4/x10 sweep, source-side modification or other sequence was run. Gaussian
   scale x1 remains the saved baseline. Stop after this authorized comparison.
