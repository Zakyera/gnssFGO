1. Decision

The user's incremental-output hypothesis is supported as an explanation for
much of the visible zigzag: chaining same-update relative motions reduces
100ms errors and excess estimated travel. But it is NOT a complete accuracy
fix. Pure chaining loses useful accumulated graph corrections, and full-path
ATE plus 1s translation/rotation RPE worsen in BOTH modes. A shorter path alone
would have given the wrong acceptance decision. Original outputs are retained.

No full sensor rerun was needed. One complete OFF and one complete ON receiver
replay reconstructed the missing same-publication pose pairs from saved
journals. Neither GLIM nor Kimera nor rosbag playback was launched.

10. Interpretation and next decision

The red line connects successive estimates from different graph solutions.
It includes local movement AND revisions between publications. Purple keeps
only same-solution movements, so it avoids many correction jumps, but does
not inherit later corrections to its past. This is a different causal output,
not merely a faithful smoother rendering of the same absolute trajectory.
Kimera's incremental flag motivated a valid test, not a guarantee that fusion's
newest-support increments have the same drift behavior as Kimera keyframes.

Recommended: do NOT replace the corrected graph estimate with this pure chain
or accept based on path length alone. Retain separate local-odometry and
corrected-graph products plus their explicit correction transform, and decide
which one each consumer needs. That decomposition is verified algebraically
here; a production publisher and covariance/velocity semantics are NOT yet
implemented or validated. No blindly copied graph covariance for chained pose.
Any future correction-injection/delay/propagation design must test both local
continuity and longer-horizon drift. It needs separate authorization; none
started. This result does not prove all model/frame/timing code bug-free.

IE-TC shares CPT7 with estimators; not independent prism GT. No nonlinear
stationarity claim, sensor rerun, Qc/default change, guard relaxation, deletion,
commit/push or new dataset/method campaign. ARC-3 remains out of scope.
