CP-V72 -- WHY THE FULL FROZEN REPLAY STOPPED
2026-10-31. Saved-data diagnosis complete; no implementation or replay.

CONCLUSION
This stop is a synchronization limitation in the new diagnostic replay harness,
not a demonstrated failure of factor retention or a numerical optimizer failure.
CP71 ran REMOVE only. Its exact-update checker correctly refused to continue
when the scheduled belief was absent from the backend's queue snapshot. The
harness preserves recorded bag availability but does not synchronize backend
readiness with callback/forwarding completion. Holding early messages solves
early admission; it does not guarantee arrival before a faster replay update.

THE RELEVANT IMPLEMENTATION
ROS poseOdomBeliefInCallback converts and buffers incoming beliefs. In parallel
mode, the wrapper calls flushExternalOdometryBeliefsToPipeline from a nominal
20Hz WALL loop. The backend independently pops its pending queue when processing
a keyframe. There is therefore a callback/forwarding boundary in addition to
rosbag publication. The current parallel_run=1 is recorded in profile/runtime.

CP70's private gate calls select immediately AFTER the pending-queue pop. It
holds already-arrived inputs whose scheduled update is in the future, but has
no readiness handshake or bounded wait for a due input. If such input is absent
at its target update, it throws before native matching/optimization. The guard
is doing its job; the scheduling harness cannot guarantee its prerequisite.
Replaying bag timestamps alone does not recreate original concurrent CPU timing.

LIMITS OF THIS DIAGNOSIS
Exact per-message callback receive, wrapper forwarding and backend-pop wall
timestamps were not all saved. We can establish the missing-at-pop event and
the asynchronous scheduling defect, not apportion exact microsecond delays
between ROS transport, callback execution and the20Hz forwarding boundary.
Bag recorder timestamps use simulated clock; the observed -1.112269ms recorder
minus scheduled bag time does not prove negative real transport latency.
No claim that a specific thread's precise lateness was measured.

RECOMMENDED NEXT CHANGE -- PROPOSAL ONLY
Add a diagnostic-only bounded readiness/acknowledgement barrier before solving
the scheduled backend update: permit actual callbacks to deliver the original
due message, confirm it is enqueued, then continue with exactly the same frozen
payload, endpoint binding and original update ID. Preserve recorded availability;
do not read future payloads early, backdate messages, move the factor to another
update, skip it, relax covariance/matching guards or add arbitrary sleeps.
Timeout or identity/order discrepancy must still fail closed. Avoid deadlock by
not holding the queue mutex or blocking the callback/forwarding worker while
waiting. Bound queue/backlog and retain ordinary+one-refresh semantics.
Validate ordering, delayed callbacks, timeout, missing/duplicate inputs and
unchanged disabled behavior before any separately authorized fresh capture.
This is conditional replay synchronization, not a new estimator or live latency
certificate. No implementation/retry was done in this diagnosis.
