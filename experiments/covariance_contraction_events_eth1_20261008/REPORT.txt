ETH1 Kimera late covariance contraction: retained-log localization
2026-10-08

Conclusion
==========
The late CBS-ON confidence increase is strongly organized by a belief's
position in the fixed-lag window. It is not merely one difficult section of
ETH1: at the SAME backend update, older beliefs typically contract faster in
ON, while the reverse is typical in OFF. The largest synchronized late
rotational contraction coincides with the first retirement of a pose that had
received a GLIM->Kimera factor.

The code gives a concrete candidate mechanism: explicit received CBS factors
are excluded from Kimera's LOCAL covariance graph, but a fixed-lag prior
formed from a CBS-influenced graph is retained. Information can therefore
enter that covariance graph through the prior when its original external
factor is no longer explicitly identifiable. This is a provenance hypothesis
supported by code and timing, NOT a quantitatively isolated cause.

Important counterevidence: ON/OFF differences and synchronous contraction
also occur BEFORE the first marginalization. Changing visual constraints and
linearization already matter. We cannot attribute all contraction to the
prior, call the Schur complement defective, or conclude that information was
counted twice from these logs alone.

2. The late confidence gain is tied to window age
================================================
Uncertainty below means sqrt(trace of the rotation/rho 3x3 marginal), not the
trace itself, a per-axis sigma, or a confidence interval.

Same 556 completed physical intervals, median per-edge ratios:

                                          OFF           ON
Last valid rotation sigma / previous      0.997812      0.894218
Last valid rho sigma / previous           1.001910      0.909800
Rotation sigma / six updates earlier      0.978863      0.660784
Rho sigma / six updates earlier           1.026670      0.643838

Thus the last valid ON revision alone reduces rotational uncertainty by
10.58% and rho uncertainty by 9.02%, typically. OFF is almost unchanged.
555/556 ON beliefs lose >5% rotational uncertainty at that last step; OFF
0/556 do. The corresponding rho fractions are 84.53% ON and 0.90% OFF.
The 5% cutoff is a descriptive reporting marker only, never a filter gate.

This is not just a final-step jump. Over the last six backend intervals
(about 1.2 s), ON loses 33.92% rotation and 35.62% rho uncertainty. Median
last-step share of positive late log-contraction is only 15.80% rotation and
12.05% rho. The process builds gradually and accelerates near the window edge.

Within-update check, holding physical backend time fixed:
For every update with >=10 eligible late beliefs, correlate their update age
with their one-step uncertainty ratio. Negative means older beliefs contract
faster at the SAME update. Across 562 updates:

                         OFF                  ON
rotation median rank     +1.000               -1.000
rho median rank          +0.877               -0.952
negative fraction rot     9.43%                95.20%
negative fraction rho    24.73%                82.74%

These are descriptive within-window statistics, not independent-trial
p-values. This controls the simple explanation that all older beliefs happen
to be in a different motion section. It does not separate their different
visual track histories from marginal-prior influence.

The important distinction:
 Before elimination, a received CBS factor is an explicit tracked object,
 so LOCAL can exclude it.
 After elimination, its influence can be mixed with native factors in the
 marginal prior. Removing remaining explicit CBS factors does not undo that
 prior. LOCAL at this point is not certified native-information-only.

At a fixed linearization, elimination of old variables z into retained y
forms Lambda_prior = H_yy - H_yz H_zz^-1 H_zy. If H contains CBS information,
the resulting prior can contain it too; the contribution is not removed by
subtracting the original measurement covariance or dropping visible slots.
This algebra describes the code's possible information path, not a new model.

Even BEFORE elimination, LOCAL is evaluated at the CBS-influenced estimate,
and its visual factors/track histories may differ from OFF. OFF vs ON therefore
does not hold either the full native graph or the linearization fixed.

The numerical checks rule against an ordinary solve/rank/jitter failure on
these payloads. The source log records 580 persistent G->K applications on
580 distinct receiver endpoint pairs (no repeated receiver edge among those
applications). This does not rule out unrelated native-factor duplication or
historical information correlation; actual full snapshots are needed for that.
The downstream receiver's stable-ID replacement was already verified and is
not reinterpreted here as accumulating multiple factors per revision.
