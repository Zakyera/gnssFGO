INTERPRETATION
At the anchor, Local(anchor,Retract(anchor,h)) has identity derivative. The
implementation nevertheless estimates this by subtracting finite-precision
poses after1e-6 perturbations. Global-position cancellation and the prior's
directional information can make this approximation exceed the guard. Thus
identical scientific settings need not guarantee that every prior passes.
This is deterministic numerical sensitivity for a given input, not proof that
the full-run failure was randomness or that a substantive cache bug is absent.
