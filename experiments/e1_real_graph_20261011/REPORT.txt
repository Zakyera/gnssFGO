1. Finding and boundary

The synchronous-compatible GPU batching candidate passed all six preselected
real-graph comparisons. Ordinary A, batched B, and ordinary repeat A2 produced
exactly equal saved augmented Hessians and complete joint covariances. All six
outgoing full 6x6 Schur-relative covariance comparisons also had zero maximum
absolute difference. Supplied LOCAL Values and main-smoother endpoint means
were unchanged. The independent post-run matrix checker passed.

This establishes equivalence on these sampled real graphs, including graphs
after fixed-lag retirement, not a proof for every possible future graph. The
production selector remains disabled. This was NOT a full-system throughput,
trajectory-equivalence or accuracy experiment. No gnssFGO receiver, EKF, GT
scoring, full-sequence run, automatic retry, E1b or E2 work was performed.

Only a private copy of cbs_bridge.cpp is instrumented. At eligible LOCAL calls
10/30/60/100/150/180 it computes A/B/A2 in place with identical real factor
pointers, slots, keys and sidecar Values. No reconstructed point-cloud factors
or reset inlier history. The compatibility path uses the existing batch kernels
while preserving synchronous cache/inlier semantics (preceding E1 report).

The ordinary A linearization remains authoritative for outgoing covariance.

as in production, while covariance conditioning comes from the LOCAL sidecar.
The candidate does not replace source estimation, conditioning or covariance
math. Diagnostic duplication/I/O can change arrival interleaving; do not call
this event-for-event identical to a separate source run.
