Cost and limits
---------------
NativeGaussianHistory.cpp:208ff currently assembles/checks/factorizes the native
Gaussian system for EACH covariance query. Reusing it for every revision avoids
new GPU/nonlinear evaluations but increases Gaussian query work from first-only
to all active revised edges. No real throughput/speedup certification; the short
synthetic test timings are not a GrandTour capacity estimate. No new caching
scheme or optimization was smuggled into this policy patch.
