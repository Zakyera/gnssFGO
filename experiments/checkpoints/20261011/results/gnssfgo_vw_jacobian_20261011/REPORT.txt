Implementation
==============
Private build/SourceWnoaModel.h adds a second GPWNOAInterpolator instance with
the existing library analytic flag. During source-factor linearization, only
its velocity and angular-rate blocks (H2,H3,H5,H6) are requested. The existing
numeric instance still computes pose derivatives H1/H4 and returns the pose
used by the residual. No analytic pose derivative or analytic returned pose is
substituted. Without a Jacobian request, evaluation remains the original path.
Exact support endpoints remain unchanged. Shared endpoint keys still sum their
contributions through the existing relative-factor chain rule.

GPWNOAPrior motion factors and their derivatives are unchanged. Prediction,
stable-ID replacement, graph topology, event chronology and solver policy are
unchanged. This inherits the previously validated private evaluation cache;
comparison uses its retained cache-only replay, not a newly rerun baseline.
