Implementation
--------------
build/FullPublicationRefinement.h is copied from the frozen WNOA build. Only its
private-polish evaluation branch is changed. All candidate solver updates, fresh
QR rebuilds, decisions, criteria and commit/rollback behavior are unchanged.

build/CachedGaussianEvaluation.h owns Values snapshots and copies the immutable
factor-slot list. It caches residuals and fresh-at-that-Values linearizations.
Cache lifetime ends inside one unchanged refinement graph; there is no cross-
revision, cross-support, global, or stale-iSAM2-Jacobian cache. Factor-pointer and
activation checks reject mismatches. Gaussian/constrained checks remain explicit.

The old inner pass linearizes the same anchor twice to calculate predicted cost
fields that are then discarded, and the same seed twice for two predictions.
The new branch calculates cumulative actual cost from cached residuals, and
calculates both local predictions from one fresh seed linearization. Actual
and predicted summation/traversal orders are preserved. Each candidate gradient
still uses its own current Values. The outer ordinary refinement is untouched.

In each inner pass: six explicit full-graph linearizations outside solver calls
become three (one seed, two candidate gradients). Residual evaluations at fixed
anchor/seed are also reused. Solver-internal work is not counted by this figure.
There are no analytic-derivative substitutions or changes to finite-difference
step sizes in the optimizer.

build/FailureProbe.h is a read-only diagnostic called only after an invalid
publication decision and after the publication timer/log are finalized. It
exports simultaneous graph Values, factor keys/types/noise/residual diagnostics,
and central differences of whitened residuals for the 20 largest native-gradient
coordinates at seven fixed steps from 1e-3 to 1e-7. It feeds nothing back to the
optimizer and does not authorize publication. This is not a full independent
stationarity certificate or a serialized, stand-alone restorable graph.

Large opposing contributions are normal near a least-squares optimum; they do

Other coordinates can cross the acceptance threshold depending on evaluation:

The second example is important: the independent check does not simply certify

the maximum remains ABOVE 1e-5 for every tested step; it is about 1.5-1.8e-5
at steps 1e-3 through 1e-5 and grows for smaller steps.

Interpretation: there is demonstrated derivative/evaluation sensitivity at the
threshold scale, including strong cancellation in J^T r. The dominant native
coordinates cluster around v1233-v1236, i.e. recent support states. This does
not identify a unique faulty routine, certify an exact derivative, measure a
full condition number, or justify relaxing the convergence threshold. The
top-20 selection is not a complete independent gradient audit. Truncation and
roundoff remain possible in these independent finite differences as well.

The next decision should distinguish two issues:
 1. Caching is a validated, modest engineering saving on this fixed journal.
 2. Numerical derivative/stationarity reliability and remaining full-history
    refinement cost still require work before a longer live experiment.
The present evidence supports checking the existing WNOA/interpolation
derivative path next, not blindly switching its analytic branch or treating
fixed-lag marginalization as a cure for this demonstrated numerical issue.
