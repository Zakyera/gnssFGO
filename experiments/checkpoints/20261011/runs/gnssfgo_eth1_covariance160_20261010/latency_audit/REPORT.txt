GLIM CBS-ON latency diagnosis — retained ETH1 160 s attempt
========================================================
Read-only diagnosis, 2026-10-10. No source/fuser rerun, profiling attachment,
estimator change, covariance change, or setting change. Only this analysis
script, its JSON output and this report are new. Original REPORT.txt and
captures remain unchanged.

Conclusion
----------
GLIM really fell behind, but the measured extra time is overwhelmingly in
CBS callbacks, not in its main native smoother update. The callbacks contain
an uncapped active-factor influence diagnostic and an auxiliary LOCAL smoother
and covariance rebuild. These perform synchronous GPU factor work on the
odometry thread. Slow processing then causes input backpressure and eventually
beliefs too old for Kimera's active alignment history.

This is stronger evidence than the earlier warning-count hypothesis. It does
not provide a CPU/GPU profiler's exact division between kernels, synchronization,
allocation, disk writes, or contention. No counterfactual speedup was measured.

What follows / boundaries
------------------------
The engineering target is redundant synchronous CBS-side computation, before
changing rates, lag, covariance, or mathematical factors. A future authorized
fix should make expensive influence diagnostics genuinely optional/capped and
measure cost of the required sidecar/marginal calculation without altering its
covariance result. Merely suppressing warnings will not eliminate GPU work.
Do not promise a speedup or output equivalence until an isolated validation.
No fix or new experiment was performed in this diagnosis.

gnssFGO was not crashed or queue-blocked in the stopped capture; rendering and
graph inspectors were already disabled. System-wide CPU/GPU utilization was
not recorded at sufficient granularity to exclude all secondary contention.
This report establishes the main timed software paths, not every hardware cause.
