Matched ETH1 40-second CBS-OFF vs temporary CBS-ON — 2026-10-08
=============================================================

Result
------
In this bounded experiment, temporary-factor CBS-ON improves native GLIM's
partial-prism ATE slightly, but worsens its 1 s RPE. Native Kimera and both
fused trajectory products are worse under ON for all three reported metrics.
This is a short-window result, not a conclusion about all ETH1 or other data.
Reducing late covariance contraction is not sufficient to improve accuracy.
No follow-up tuning, method changes or additional experiment was performed.
