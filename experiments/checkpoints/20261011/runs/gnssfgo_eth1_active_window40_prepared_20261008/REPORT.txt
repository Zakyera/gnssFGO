==========================================================

Outcome
-------
One authorized 40 s CBS-ON source experiment completed with concurrent native
GLIM, native Kimera and the frozen Gaussian, no-central-IMU WNOA receiver.
No new OFF/persistent-ON run, full sequence, tuning or estimator modification.
The historical active_window_temporary mode actually inserts and retires
external factors in both sources. The receiver's exact captured-journal replay
is byte-identical for all nine checked products, including online and final
states/trajectories. Frozen configuration/payload/causality checks pass via the

Important finding: Kimera's strong late-window covariance contraction under
persistent CBS is largely absent in this temporary-factor pilot. However its
revised relative means do not thereby become uniformly more accurate. Do not
promote this mode as a validated accuracy improvement or production default.

Validation-label issue (not hidden)
-----------------------------------
The frozen validate_case.py ran once, including the authorized journal repeat.
It passed configuration, covariance conservation, accounting, causality,
stationarity, throughput and all nine exact-repeat comparisons, then failed
its final Kimera removal-label assertion: it expected remove_committed.

Code inspection establishes the normal Kimera BPSAM path logs remove_scheduled
and erases its tracking entry while passing the real removeFactorIndices into
the solver. The later bookkeeping commit skips that already-erased entry and
does not emit a second removal log. Thus the assertion selected the wrong log
path; it was not evidence of a numerical estimator failure.

No frozen file was edited. check_lifecycle.py independently reconciles all
tracked slots/removals. validate_completed_capture.py is an isolated copy with
the correct label, requiring the independent reconciliation and rechecking all
original criteria. It REUSES the completed repeat read-only: no additional
solver/source rerun. It passed. pilot_on_validation.json explicitly names this
validation authority; pilot_on_validation_supplement.json retains the result.
validation_label_mismatch.txt records exact source lines and the failed assertion.

The first scoring invocation stopped before scoring because the original
checker had not written pilot_on_validation.json. Its empty output directory
was retained as pilot_evaluation_prevalidation_empty. The supplementary result
was then supplied under the expected filename with explicit provenance, and
unchanged scoring completed. No scientific failure was tuned away or retried.

What this establishes / does not establish
----------------------------------------
Operational compatibility: supported for this bounded pilot, with explicit
slot removal, preserved downstream payloads, causal stationary receiver and
deterministic replay. The late covariance-contraction behavior changes strongly
in the expected direction under the historical temporary mode.

Not established: a pure lifetime-only causal attribution, calibrated source
uncertainty, complete removal of indirect CBS information from marginal priors,
better general accuracy, independent sources, or performance on another window.
Means/linearizations/native factor history can retain indirect effects even
after explicit external factors are removed. Forced relinearization/full solve
is an additional implementation difference. Do not silently make it default.
