Seven-second lifecycle simulation
--------------------------------
At every support boundary, after pending dispatch, retire supports STRICTLY
older than current physical support minus 7 s. Equality remains active.
The first retirement is at support 71 / 7.1 s. The simulation rejects pending
old brackets, revisions after absorption, missing support keys and inconsistent
ID outcomes; it does not drop late factors, coalesce revisions or pin history.

Result: zero added exclusions, zero missing brackets, zero revisions to
absorbed IDs. All 86,053 replacements remain possible. At the end:
  2,201 IDs = 2,101 absorbed + 100 still explicit.
  GLIM: 1,410 absorbed + 66 explicit.
  Kimera: 691 absorbed + 34 explicit.
  1,410 supports retired; 71 remain; 1,410 retirement boundaries.
