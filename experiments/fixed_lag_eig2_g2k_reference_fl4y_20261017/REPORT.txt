FL4y — exact GLIM-to-Kimera interval check against reference
2026-10-17 | retained EIG-2 FL4x and SPX-3 FL4w
==========================================================

Conclusion
----------
EIG-2's injected GLIM translational relative beliefs are LESS accurate than
Kimera's CBS-OFF internal relative estimate on the exact receiver intervals.
SPX-3 shows the opposite ordering by a large margin. This is a concrete,
reference-backed distinction between these windows, not an inference from
whole-trajectory ATE or covariance trace.

Most EIG-2 translation error is already present in the incoming raw GLIM
belief before temporal alignment. A hypothetical perfect GT bridge barely
changes its aggregate translation RMS. No evidence here supports blaming
temporal alignment as the main translational error source.

The complete Kimera ON update often improves the already-CBS-affected
pre-update relative estimate, yet its post-update translation remains worse
than matched OFF. Thus 'each external factor makes the receiver worse' is
NOT supported. This audit does not isolate an individual factor's causal
effect from the rest of an update, or prove why raw GLIM beliefs have their
observed errors. No code/model/covariance settings were modified.

What was compared
-----------------
All292 actual G2K injections in the EIG-2 capture and289 in SPX-3.
Each interval is about0.200s; no ATE alignment, no newly optimized graph,
no trajectory-derived proxy for an incoming belief, no cherry-picked errors.
The same physical receiver interval is used for the aligned measurement,
Kimera pre-update, Kimera post-update and OFF internal controls.

GLIM raw means are the actual incoming body-frame payloads logged BEFORE
the temporal bridge (after the upstream frame path), not raw LiDAR scans.
They are evaluated against GT on their own sender intervals. Aligned and
frozen inserted means match exactly. Receiver covariances are unchanged.

Kimera pre-injection means come from the preceding-update smoother poses
in KIMERA_CBS_REVISION_EDGE_ROW, NOT native ROS incremental odometry or
later/finalized poses. Code records these poses from calculateEstimate.
They reproduce all recorded pre-injection residuals and the independently
logged pre-injection matrices (see validation below). OFF controls use the
same physical endpoints and the same update index; OFF/ON timestamps match.
Post-update means use that same update's smoother snapshot. The update
includes all normal factors and retirements, not only the new external one.

Reference and coverage
----------------------
Dataset IE-TC /boxi/inertial_explorer/tc/odometry, world enu_origin,
child cpt7_imu. This GNSS/INS reference shares the CPT7 IMU; it is NOT an
independent prism ground truth, and its own uncertainty is not modeled here.
Its short-interval orientation errors/clock/systematics can affect results.
Every sender/receiver endpoint is bracketed; maximum used gap5ms.
Translation is linearly interpolated; rotation uses shortest-path SO(3)
interpolation, equivalent to the existing evaluator's quaternion SLERP.
Integer-nanosecond source/receiver endpoints retained. No clock fitting.

EIG-2 receiver support1730638817.763483017–1730638876.162965643;
SPX-3 receiver support1730565903.672592179–1730565962.572062520.
These cover ALL accepted G2K factors in each60s capture. This is an edge
audit, not a replacement for the completed whole-journey ATE tables.
The diagnostic stays in CPT7 body coordinates because that is the factor
frame. Existing ATE/RPE at the prism lever arm are not modified or relabeled.

For a transform Z and reference Zgt:
  translation error = norm(t_Z - t_Zgt), in metres;
  rotation error = norm(Log(R_Z^T R_Zgt)), in degrees.
The SE(3) rho error is retained separately in JSON; it is not substituted
silently for Cartesian translation difference. No global rigid fit or scale.

Temporal alignment isolation
----------------------------
Let source endpoints be sa,sb and receiver endpoints ra,rb:
  Lgt = Tgt(ra)^-1 Tgt(sa)
  Rgt = Tgt(sb)^-1 Tgt(rb)
  Z_oracle_bridge = Lgt * Z_raw * Rgt.
This keeps the actual raw mean and changes ONLY the diagnostic bridge to
ground-truth motion. It was never injected into an estimator. With a perfect
raw belief this identity reproduces receiver GT (checked <1e-12).

EIG-2 median endpoint offset magnitude16.457ms; SPX-3 about16.59ms.
All physical endpoints precede/equal the logged causal update limit.

GLIM payload. This is a frozen-payload decomposition, NOT a claim about what
a closed-loop rerun with a different bridge would produce.

Interpretation limits and next decision
--------------------------------------
The records support examining source relative-belief quality before choosing
receiver weights: low native GLIM ATE does not guarantee superior0.2s
relative beliefs at their actual publication stage. A fixed 'GLIM always
healthy,Kimera always unhealthy' interpretation fails on these intervals.
The incoming EIG-2 translation is not a consistently better corrective signal.
Stronger external constraints can therefore change a locally more accurate
Kimera estimate toward a compromise; the present audit does not quantify
the resulting accumulated global bias or isolate downstream fusion causality.

Existing optimization fallbacks/cost nonincrease do not prove convergence,
and the current test neither clears every solver issue nor diagnoses one.
No covariance was changed, calibrated or compared to a chi-square cutoff.
Temporary factor removal does not make all means/linearization histories
independent. No new weighting, robust loss, source or receiver experiment.
