CP-V37 — EIG-2 CBS-OFF native-factor relinearization ablation
2026-10-29. Comparison COMPLETE; no live repair or accuracy rescore.

DECISION
========
Refreshing current-state linearization is sufficient to greatly reduce the
observed internal IMU inconsistency at these saved checkpoints, WITHOUT stronger
weights, explicit-landmark substitution, damping or repeated optimization.
All seven single native-factor steps reduce maximum IMU position residual,
maximum IMU rotation/velocity residual and native total reported cost.
Re-solving the original stored-theta equations leaves the bad behavior nearly
unchanged. This substantially strengthens the retained-linearization hypothesis
and removes the CP-V36 joint-landmark/damping/iteration confounds for this effect.

It does NOT identify which factor family's relinearization is essential, prove
the origin of the baseline problem, establish nonlinear stationarity, or show a
smooth/accurate live trajectory. Original prior-coordinate transport and native
factor refresh are tested together. Smart retriangulation/validity decisions
can change through the unchanged native policy. No production fix is installed.

EXACT SCOPE
===========
Seven CP-V36 after-solve checkpoints:800,934,1000,1335,1336,1337,1338.
Control: native Gaussian graph at original stored theta, one direct solve.
Treatment: all native factors linearized at the captured current estimate,
one undamped Gaussian solve and one retraction. Same explicit key ordering and
native Cholesky elimination in both branches; not incremental tree ordering.
No new measurements, source/receiver playback, GLIM, incoming CBS or CBS-ON.
No repeated nonlinear iteration, line search, damping, weight/guard tuning or retry.
The single relinearize/solve/retract operation is one Gauss-Newton step; the
results.json nonlinear_iterations=0 field denotes no additional iterative loop,
not an absence of that explicitly recorded single step.

ALL985--1133 nonnull native factors retained per checkpoint, including all
932--1080 native smart stereo factors and unchanged IMU/bias factors. Original
smart observations/calibrations/noise/parameters/caches are cloned, not manually
cleared or forced valid. Native triangulation/validity rules are retained.
The original marginal Gaussian and owning anchor remain immutable. The existing
OriginalChartPrior adapter evaluates exactly the original retained-prior
function and transports its Jacobian into the current state coordinates. It
does not restore eliminated nonlinear factors or reanchor the prior function.

CONTROL / MATHEMATICAL CHECKS
============================
Fresh original equations at stored theta equal the saved cache exactly for all
seven checkpoints (relative difference0). Directly solving them reproduces the
captured estimate within maximum0.000036665m pose translation and0.000091862deg
rotation. Thus more accurate back-substitution of the same old model is not the
explanation for the large treatment improvement. Original residual .827052806m
becomes .827052680m in the stored control, versus .027509880m after refresh.

Both branches solve their own Gaussian equations accurately: maximum scaled
normal-equation residual1.650e-10; refreshed branch maximum1.040e-10. These are
LINEAR solve checks, not nonlinear gradient/stationarity certificates.
Forty-two finite-state prior energy/directional-derivative checks pass on the
real checkpoints, including theta/current and finite perturbations. Maximum
energy relative error0; derivative tolerances fixed in PROTOCOL.txt before data.
Release and ASan/UBSan synthetic native-smart/IMU/marginal fixtures pass for
original and deliberately displaced estimates. Linked vendor DSOs are not
sanitized. Every input snapshot serializes byte-identically before/after use.
Independent output accounting confirms same slots/factor types/counts, cost
sums, and captured costs matching CP-V36. Full details in results.json.

============================

steps on a dependency assertion expecting unused libcbs.so. ELF/ldd establish
normal linker --as-needed omission. PREFLIGHT_REPAIR.txt records the correction;
failed.json/run_ablation.py/restoration.json remain historical. continue_saved.py
checked actual original pinned GTSAM dependencies and ran each NEVER-started
checkpoint once using the unchanged executable. No source or numerical retry.
finished.json and continuation_restoration.json are authoritative completion.
Both initially stopped containers restored; no source/production build touched.
Final hashes, original Hesai checkpoint and RRD checks are in closeout.json.

NEXT DECISION — NOT IMPLEMENTED OR LAUNCHED
=========================================
Do not strengthen priors or proceed to CBS-ON from this result. A useful next
gate would be a private incremental OFF integration of original-prior-preserving
refresh with native factors, first tested on synthetic marginalization,
fixed-key ownership, cache/tree, covariance and rollback invariants. A bounded
live test would require a separate decision after those gates. The large late
corrections and remaining residuals preclude calling this a ready online fix.
