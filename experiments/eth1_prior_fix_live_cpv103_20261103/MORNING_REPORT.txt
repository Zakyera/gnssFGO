matrices and exact theta were not recorded, so they cannot now be reconstructed

not a claim that deletion of old runs caused the failure.

when the original Gaussian and cached prior are identical, finite-difference
chart conversion at an exactly unchanged anchor can exceed the existing 1e-8
preservation threshold. A synthetic anisotropic prior at CP99's last published
pose produced converted/cache error 1.5081297469405463e-8 while original/cache
error was zero. This reproduces the exception mechanism, not CP99's actual
failed matrix, theta or branch. CP98 saved priors and synthetic stress cases are
kept distinct in the diagnostic reports.

PRECISE CORRECTION — NO SCIENTIFIC SETTINGS RELAXATION
OriginalChartPrior first validates the values. When every relevant value equals
its immutable anchor at ZERO tolerance, it clones the original stored Gaussian
instead of numerically differentiating an identity chart change. If any relevant
value moved, the old finite-difference conversion branch remains unchanged.
NativePriorRefresh still checks original/cache AND converted/cache at 1e-8,
with unchanged key/ownership, lock and all-before-commit transaction rules.
There is no epsilon match, anchor reset, noise/weight change or removed prior.

A typed failure now retains the slot, keys, separate errors, original/cache/
converted matrices and exact typed anchor. The new backend's failure-only
recorder saves unique archives and rethrows the original exception. Disabled or
invalid recording paths do not suppress the fatal guard. The archive format is
native x86_64, not a cross-platform or power-loss durability guarantee.
