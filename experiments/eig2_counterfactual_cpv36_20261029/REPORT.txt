DECISION
========
The leading problem is nonlinear consistency of the modified fixed-lag Kimera
baseline, not a failure to solve its stored linear system. Stronger prior
weights are NOT justified as the first repair by this evidence.

At the267.8s checkpoint, the captured incremental state satisfies its cached
linear equations very accurately, but has a native IMU position-constraint
residual as large as0.827m. An isolated, derivative-tested joint-landmark solve
with the same measurements/noise and unchanged original-chart marginal prior
reduces that residual to1.05e-6m. Re-evaluating native factors at the resulting
candidate also reduces their reported cost from1.84364e9 to5469.895.
This shows much more internally consistent candidate states exist without
strengthening the prior. It does NOT establish micrometre positioning accuracy,
an all-native-factor stationary solution, or a working online trajectory fix.

The difficult solves are bounded/nonconverged and visual activity changes.
The comparator differs in damping/iterations, current-state linearization and
explicit landmark treatment together; it does not isolate prior refresh alone.
Interpret these results as mechanism evidence, not a deployable replacement.
