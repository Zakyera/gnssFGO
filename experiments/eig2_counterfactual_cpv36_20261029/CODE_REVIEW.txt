Read-only scope note, before scientific checkpoint analysis
=========================================================
The captured CP-K9 wrapper calls cpk9PromotePriorCharts only inside the
enabled AND nonempty scheduled temporary-peer-removal branch. The private
incremental_fixed_lag_bpsam_smoother.h lines232--239 show this condition.
NativePriorRefresh.h and the original CP-K9 REPORT.txt explicitly document
that ordinary OFF preserves the old path. Thus CPK9_PRIOR_REFRESH=1 is NOT
evidence of per-cycle nonlinear prior refresh in OFF. No incoming factor or
retirement means no activation of this conversion.

The new live hook only clones and serializes. It does not promote/unlock,
reanchor, optimize, or feed its independently reconstructed graph back.
The offline OriginalChartPrior retains the captured Gaussian and its original
anchor. Its coordinate Jacobian is evaluated at the current trial state.
This tests an alternative nonlinear treatment of the retained approximation;
it does not recover eliminated nonlinear information or certify a live fix.

Smart-factor caveat: the conditional explicit-landmark objective is initialized
with the original smart-factor triangulated/cached points and cost. It retains
the measurements and noise but fixes which visual factors are initially active
for each starting point. Native activity after the solve and landmark distance
policy violations are counted separately. The two starts need not have the
same initial active set. Native and joint final objective values are distinct.
No result may be called an all-native-factor optimum merely because its joint
cost decreases or its bounded optimizer returns.
