----------------------

Kimera YAML did NOT keep the historical Kimera optimization behavior unchanged.

solves the different temporary-CBS ON instability that motivated the wrapper.

Pause CP-K2's proposed joint-landmark redesign. The next useful control is the
ordinary incremental backend, not another new landmark solver. Production and
frozen run settings remain unchanged pending that separately authorized control.

4. Why simply removing the wrapper is not yet the full solution
--------------------------------------------------------------
Retained FL4m/FL4n/FL4p evidence concerns a DIFFERENT problem in temporary CBS-ON:
- FL4m uses450 identical already-aligned incoming GLIM factors. Remove vs retain
  is identical before retirement, then different histories develop. The harmful
  update438 is present with removal; retaining the factors avoids that large
  event. Retain is a diagnostic, NOT exact historical persistent CBS.
- FL4n at three checkpoints finds no missing retired factor, bad cache assembly
  or erroneous linear solve: fresh-at-the-same-theta, cached and tree equations
  agree. Do not repeat this check without new evidence.
- FL4p at update89 finds a downhill direction with an oversized full step,
  dominated by an old-anchor native IMU approximation. Current-point IMU
  derivatives agree with numerical derivatives. Most pose anchors are fixed.

W/src/gtsam/gtsam/nonlinear/ISAM2.cpp:739-741 fixes linearization points incident
to marginal priors; ISAM2-impl.h:382-387 excludes those keys even on full solve.
The estimated states are NOT fixed. CBS bpsam.cpp:4163-4167,4210-4233 forces
re-elimination/full solve for active-window CBS changes, but does not bypass
fixed anchor exclusion. This explains why correct old-chart equations need
not yield a good finite nonlinear step. It is not proof of a wrong IMU formula.

Therefore two problems must not be merged:
 (a) temporary removal/prior-history sensitivity and old-anchor steps in ON;
 (b) our later universal nonlinear acceptance/fallback incompatibility in OFF.
The second is not evidence that the first was imaginary. CP-K2's standalone
infinite-depth candidate is a third, rejected diagnostic, not native VIO behavior.

CP-I2 already validates explicit native-information prior provenance for the
temporary/remove policy at fixed charts. Preserve it: switching to persistent
would change the intended information lifecycle, not merely fix a launch typo.
Neither LOCAL provenance nor removal proves statistical independence.
