CP-V110 — EIG-2 regional late-revision intervention, 2026-11-04

OUTCOME
The controlled test works, but the tested change does NOT solve the fusion
degradation. Freezing eligible late Kimera covariance improves conventional
ONLINE ATE by only 0.8155%; freezing means worsens it 4.1054%, and freezing
both worsens it 2.6717%. With the original ON alignment frozen, covariance-only
whole-trajectory error is slightly worse. Local benefits redistribute error;
they do not remove the main ON penalty. No production policy was changed.

INTERPRETATION AND NEXT STEP
Do not deploy a blanket late-covariance or mean freeze based on this result.
This rejects the sufficiency of this particular late regional intervention,
not every covariance/correlation explanation. Earlier beliefs, other regions,
directional weighting, accumulated source bias and prior feedback remain.
Saved source beliefs already contain their live CBS history; changing receiver
inputs does not remove that history or test a feedback-free source system.
Split IMUs do not establish independence. Alpha noise is experimental, not
certified calibration. IE-TC shares CPT7 and is not independent prism truth.
Payload and incremental replay parity do not certify nonlinear stationarity,
uncertainty consistency or estimator health. This is not a NEES/NIS test.

Recommended next bounded work is saved-data inspection of first/young belief
means and directional residual/weight contributions before2.5s, tracing how
drift accumulates through the identified windows. No further intervention or
live run is started or implied by this recommendation.
