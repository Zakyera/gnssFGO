FL4m — isolated Kimera CBS retirement comparison
2026-10-15 | COMPLETE: retirement/prior-history mechanism implicated

Finding
-------
With exactly the same frozen incoming GLIM factors and ETH1 sensors, removing
expired external factors before marginalization reproduces the harmful late
Kimera optimization step. Keeping those factors through marginalization avoids
that late translation instability in this bounded comparison.

This narrows the cause to the retirement intervention and its accumulated
optimization/prior history. It does NOT prove that prior inheritance is the
correct CBS production policy, identify an exact implementation defect, or
separate immediate constraint removal from the subsequent prior-history effect.
No production repair or policy change is promoted.

Controlled experiment
---------------------
Two Kimera-only runs, ETH1 original first94s, playback0.10x. No GLIM, gnssFGO,
visualizer, covariance tuning, additional sequence or automatic data-run retry.
Front stereo/CPT7, copied FL4l profile and existing native source solver remain.

A (remove): current active_window_temporary retirement, remove expiring external
factor slots before the update/marginalization.
B (retain): same active_window_temporary admission, forced solve, guards and
recovery; skip only scheduled deletion of expiring external factors. They remain
in that optimization and ordinary marginalization, then obsolete tracker entries
are removed. This is NOT a switch to the historical persistent configuration,
which also changes admission/recovery/forced-solve behavior.

450 actually constructed FL4l G->K factors are frozen AFTER native-IMU mean
alignment. Their means, all36 covariance entries, sender IDs, original recorded
availability times and physical receiver endpoint times are preserved. Existing
ROS bag playback injects them; mean realignment is disabled identically in both
cases to avoid changing measurements as the Kimera state changes. Original
unused wire candidates are omitted. This is conditional frozen-input replay,
NOT live bidirectional CBS or an exact historical closed-loop reproduction.

Both cases have454 native ROS odometry poses spanning93.599489763s, with all450
factor payloads, post-binding means/covariances, receiver endpoints and original
backend update IDs validated. Each has426 retirement events and zero recovery
rollbacks/drops. The first28 complete native messages (excluding receipt time)
and original-chart objective fields are EXACTLY identical before retirement.
First retirement: update28, diagnostic time9.755345821s; divergence then starts
small. The result is not a sudden catastrophic jump at the first retirement.

Decision
--------
The bounded intervention supports a causal role for the temporary-retirement
path/history in this late instability; the hypothesis is no longer based only on
historical ATE. It does not show that all CBS problems are solved, prove GLIM's
changes harmless everywhere, or justify reintroducing information inheritance.
If continuing, target a numerically consistent, safeguarded retirement/update
while preserving the intended no-inheritance semantics, not covariance tuning
or a new full-sequence sweep. That repair remains unimplemented. STOP.
