INTERPRETATION AND NEXT DECISION
CP105's late-revision confidence/error mismatch now has an intervention result:
changing just that weighting schedule substantially improves the global
trajectory fit in this saved stream. Random source outcomes or changed event
timing cannot explain the difference between these two replays: all such
inputs were held fixed and baseline reproduction was exact.

This does NOT prove that every later covariance is wrong, that the initial
matrix is calibrated, that covariance contraction alone is universally bad,
or that persistent peer feedback is the uniquely established source of the
problem. The first matrix can also be miscalibrated; different IMUs do not
eliminate cross-source/overlapping-belief correlation. The intervention changes
all6x6 directions, not translation trace alone. A principled revision/correlation
policy needs separate development and validation; no production setting was
changed or promoted. The earlier Kimera exact-anchor prior-preservation fix
is a distinct issue and was neither changed nor reverted.

ONLINE jitter remains: nearly unchanged path excess and short-distance RPE
despite much better ATE. Further investigation should treat current-state
prediction/publication behavior separately from covariance generation and
revision weighting. No extra ablation, new sequence, source run or replay is
automatically authorized by this result.

IE-TC shares CPT7 with GLIM and is not independent Leica/prism ground truth.
These scores do not certify true covariance calibration, nonlinear stationarity
or estimator health. The missing right image203.898841154s and inherited source
keyframe gaps/tail remain in the fixed saved source record.
