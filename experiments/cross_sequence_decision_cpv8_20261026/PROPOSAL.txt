CP-V8 PROPOSAL — one gravity-separated G2K information ablation
==============================================================
Status: design recommendation ONLY. No implementation, build or experiment
authorized/executed by this report. Keep the baseline and CP-V6A unchanged.

DECISION
The next useful intervention is NOT another scalar covariance multiplier.
Test whether removing only the two GLIM->Kimera tilt residual components,
while retaining the gravity-axis rotation and three translation components,
reduces accumulated Kimera orientation damage without losing the useful
collaboration. Keep Kimera->GLIM and downstream fusion unchanged.

This is a parameter-free STRUCTURAL ABLATION, not a proposed universal policy
that LiDAR should never constrain tilt. It is the smallest interpretable next
experiment that follows from the cross-sequence directional finding and the
failed whole-rotation variance10 intervention. It is not an adaptive gate.
If it succeeds, it justifies developing selective directional trust. If it
fails, do not respond by sweeping tilt multipliers or adding another gate.

WHY THIS, RATHER THAN MORE SCALING?
1. GLIM incoming tilt RMS is worse than the verified Kimera pre-update state
   in six of seven ON pairs; heading RMS is better in five. This is not merely
   Leica-2 or merely Velodyne. Exact physical-interval matching across sensors
   preserves the major findings for SPX-3 and LEICA-2.
2. Whole-rotation variance10 reduced some tilt/local errors but harmed heading
   and global position. It weakened useful and harmful components together.
3. Actual covariance/error ranking is too inconsistent to justify selecting
   an online gate or calibration coefficient from these records. EIG-2
   Velodyne's smaller-tilt-uncertainty selector frequently chooses the less
   accurate source. A disagreement magnitude alone cannot identify the culprit.
4. A one-way K->G-only experiment would remove helpful G->K heading and
   translation too. The proposed ablation isolates a narrower pathway.
5. Hesai EIG-2 is a counterexample: incoming GLIM tilt is slightly healthier
   in aggregate, yet Kimera long-horizon rotation worsens. The hypothesis is
   incomplete; the ablation must be falsifiable and cannot be called a fix.

MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATION TO REVIEW BEFORE ANY CODE
Let r(x)=[phi;rho] be the EXISTING six-dimensional factor residual in its
actual right-local chart, with the existing dense SPD covariance C.
Let g be a unit gravity direction expressed in that same residual chart.
Use only the receiving estimator's pre-injection state/gravity and the
measurement's nominal endpoint orientation to obtain g. Never use the IE-TC
reference, GT heading, a future state, or a hard-coded covariance index.
Derive the chart mapping against the real factor implementation first.

Freeze g when the factor is installed, for its existing temporary lifetime.
Define the full-row-rank 4x6 projection

       M = [ g^T   0(1x3) ]
           [ 0(3x3) I(3)  ].

Use the four-dimensional residual y=M r(x), covariance C_y=M C M^T,
and cost y^T C_y^-1 y (with the existing factor's cost normalization).
Keep the full 4x4 covariance, including gravity-axis/translation cross terms.
Use the projected Jacobian M J. Replace the original six-dimensional external
factor for this ablation; never insert both and double count the measurement.

This M refers to gravity-axis angular error, not an exact global Euler-yaw
measurement. At finite rotations these notions differ. Translation means
the retained EXISTING rho residual coordinates; SE(3) coupling remains.

This is marginalization of the discarded residual components under the local
Gaussian noise model, NOT conditioning them to zero. Do not delete rows from
the precision matrix, diagonalize C, retain an arbitrary covariance corner,
or set a zero residual while keeping six-dimensional whitening.

For fixed M and SPD C, C_y is SPD and
  M^T (M C M^T)^-1 M <= C^-1
in the positive-semidefinite ordering. Thus the projected Gaussian adds no
information relative to the original six-dimensional measurement. This does
NOT prove nonlinear solver stability, calibrated noise, independent sources
or unchanged translation/heading optimizer influence. Marginalization changes
the dense conditional coupling; it intentionally removes information.

The companion design_check.json tests only this linear-algebra specification.
Any implementation still needs exact chart/Jacobian/lifecycle/default-parity
checks on the real factor, including non-axis-aligned gravity and dense cross
blocks. Do not rebuild the entire estimator or redesign its optimizer.

ISOLATION CONTRACT
- Private opt-in at the Kimera G2K receiving factor only; default exactly off.
- Preserve original incoming/outgoing payloads and all-LOCAL exports.
- K2G unchanged; receiver WNOA model, covariances, priors, supports and lag
  unchanged. No simultaneous speed, sensor, startup, alignment or EOF changes.
- Existing temporary-factor retirement, IDs and prior-refresh rules unchanged.
- Log raw C, M, applied C_y, exact binding, pre/post states and timing.
- Do not claim this repairs the known source mean-only covariance alignment.
  Treat the ablation under the same approximation; derive its chart explicitly.

BOUNDED FUTURE TEST, ONLY AFTER SEPARATE USER APPROVAL
First: focused mathematical/implementation checks; stop if they fail.
Then: ONE full LEICA-2 Velodyne ON at the frozen 0.20x/window, fresh outputs.
Use saved OFF and original ON controls; do not rerun OFF automatically.
A full window is appropriate because the negative intervention improved1s
RPE but degraded accumulated heading over many tens of seconds. A passing
30/60s clip is not sufficient for this hypothesis.

Predeclared provisional screening criteria, on identical supported timestamps:
- At least20% lower Kimera ATE AND20s rotation RPE than original ON.
  Current CP-V8 common values2.5853m/3.3257deg imply about2.0683m/2.6606deg.
- No worsening of accumulated heading or10/20s gravity-axis angular error;
  check fixed-start anchoring at0,+1,+5s where supported.
- GLIM ATE no more than10% worse and ONLINE fusion ATE no more than5% worse;
  no >10% regression in their10/20s translation RPE. Retain all other metrics.
- No numerical/lifecycle failure, materially reduced coverage, unaccounted
  factor, protected-setting drift or workload exceeding the frozen rate budget.

These percentages are proposed PRACTICAL DECISION MARGINS, not statistical
confidence limits or covariance calibration constants. They are set before
seeing candidate results and must not be relaxed afterward. Passing is a
mechanism-screen result, NOT recovery to OFF accuracy: Leica Kimera OFF ATE
is1.0942m and20s rotation RPE1.0367deg, substantially better still.

Stop and report after this one screen. If it fails, retain the evidence and
reject this intervention; do not silently tune another strength. If it passes,
the next separately approved finite confirmation would be a matched fresh
baseline/candidate repeat (GLIM preprocessing is not bitwise deterministic),
then Hesai EIG-2 (useful-tilt counterexample) and Velodyne EIG-2 (fusion
regression). These are known regression cases, not unseen holdouts. No automatic
campaign or promotion. Generalization later requires genuinely unused data.

WHAT THIS PROPOSAL WILL NOT SOLVE OR CERTIFY
ARC-5 failed with CBS OFF, so a G2K receiving change cannot logically establish
that receiver's numerical robustness. ONLINE path inflation/delayed differences
also remain a separate output-quality issue. Shared CPT7/source correlation,
mean-only covariance alignment, initialization/gaps and uncertain IE-TC
reference are unchanged. None is silently declared fixed by better ATE.

Ultimately the production objective is a measured OFF->ON improvement without
material native-source harm across the intended domain, with safe completion
and adequate coverage. This one ablation tests a specific information pathway;
it is not a promise that the complete algorithm is solved.
