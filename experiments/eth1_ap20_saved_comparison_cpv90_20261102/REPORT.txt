CP-V90 — ETH1 split-IMU CBS-OFF, independent Leica/AP20 position check
2026-11-02 | Saved-data analysis of the completed CP-V89 capture

BOTTOM LINE
The GLIM–Kimera position-accuracy gap remains when the reference is raw Leica
total-station tracking of the AP20 prism, rather than IE-TC. On exactly the same
3,873 measurement timestamps, GLIM RMSE is 0.031474 m and Kimera is 0.684862 m.
On that same mask versus IE-TC they are 0.032814 m and 0.686583 m. IE-TC versus
AP20 is 0.010988 m. Thus this observed gap is not explained by choosing IE-TC
as the reference on these supported intervals. This does NOT prove independence
of every error source, certify IMU calibration, or establish the cause of
Kimera's remaining error. It does not qualify split-body CBS/fusion operation.

SCOPE AND METHOD
GLIM: Hesai LiDAR + CPT7/HG4930. Kimera: front Alphasense stereo + built-in
Alphasense IMU, using the experimental CP88/89 bias random walks. CBS OFF in
both. No fusion. No estimator rerun, sensor replay, build or parameter change.
The CP89 Method 1 first/newest absolute outputs are used: GLIM published
absolute poses and Kimera absolute backend poses, NOT its native integrated
incremental trajectory and NOT later finalized/revised historical poses.

All positions refer to the SAME physical prism point. CP89 immutable TUM files
already include each estimator's body-to-prism lever arm; it is NOT applied
again. Raw IE-TC CPT7 poses are converted using the pinned T_cpt7_prism.
The raw AP20 bag supplies measured PointStamped positions in leica_total_station.
Timestamp and position values are retained; no AP20 gap filling or smoothing.

EVO 1.36.3 APE translation RMSE, one SE(3) rigid alignment per comparison using
all accepted positions; no scale optimization, time-offset fit or per-fragment
realignment. Estimator positions are linearly interpolated at raw AP20 times
only across saved native gaps <=0.5s, never extrapolated. IE-TC interpolation
uses gaps <=0.05s. All five comparisons share the exact same accepted mask.
No samples are selected or rejected according to estimation error.
Identity quaternions merely serialize position-only curves in TUM format.
No orientation accuracy or RPE is claimed from these Leica observations.

INTERPRETATION LIMITS / NEXT DECISION
This is a useful independent positional cross-check, not an official ETH1
benchmark result, full temporal coverage, attitude evaluation or uncertainty
calibration. IE-TC shares CPT7 with GLIM; the raw Leica measurements do not use
the estimators' IMU propagation. Agreement here cannot establish absence of
correlation everywhere or certify either reference over its unobserved gaps.
The evidence supports retaining GLIM's good positional accuracy on this mask
and treating Kimera's larger error as a remaining estimator/configuration issue
to investigate, not dismissing it as an IE-TC-reference artifact. It does NOT
identify whether Alpha hardware, its experimental noise model, visual tracking,
initialization or another issue is responsible. No new experiment authorized
or launched by this report; existing run and covariance conclusions unchanged.
