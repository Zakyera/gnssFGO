ETH1 Gaussian GLIM + Kimera + WNOA covariance-quality audit
2026-10-08

Result
======
The most specific new finding is a late-revision confidence gain in Kimera
CBS-ON that is not matched by a similar reduction of relative-reference
discrepancy. After age 2 s, median reported rotation root-sum uncertainty falls
51.0% and translation root-sum uncertainty falls 47.3%, while discrepancy falls
only 0.6% and 5.4%, respectively, on the SAME 571 physical beliefs.

This is a concrete lead for examining covariance provenance/accumulation in
later source revisions. It is not proof of a Schur-complement bug, a calibrated
overconfidence factor, a reason to disable revisions, or authorization to tune.
The original Gaussian scale-1, no-central-IMU WNOA implementation is unchanged.

2. Meaning and limits of the measurements
========================================
The existing audited helper is reused, unchanged:
  /home/yeranis/results/gnssfgo_eth1_covariance_audit_20260930/covariance_age_reference.py
  reference cache: sibling ie_tc.npz

Reference: /boxi/inertial_explorer/tc/odometry, cpt7_imu frame. Interpolate at
the exact belief endpoint timestamps with the existing 50 ms bracket gate.
Residual: e = Log(Z_source^-1 T_reference_relative), ordering [phi,rho].
rho is the SE(3) tangent translation, not a substituted Cartesian residual.
Every accepted interval is evaluable. No trajectory alignment is fitted here.

Reported channel uncertainty below is sqrt(trace(Sigma_channel)): a root-sum
variance, NOT a per-axis standard deviation, covariance trace, or 95% radius.
Errors are norms of the corresponding reference-discrepancy coordinates.

IE-TC shares CPT7 data and lacks joint relative-reference covariance. These
are descriptive discrepancies, not true-error observations with known noise.
Whitened values are NOT calibrated NIS/NEES/chi-square coverage or proof that
all discrepancy belongs to GLIM/Kimera. Constant reference/systematic errors
can create an error floor while internally computed covariance contracts.
Individual posterior covariance contraction does not require the realised
error of every estimate to decrease. The aggregate pattern warrants scrutiny,
but does not identify its cause on its own.

3. Does uncertainty rank better and worse beliefs?
=================================================
Latest version at common cutoff; one observation per physical interval.
Positive Spearman correlation means larger reported uncertainty tends to
accompany larger discrepancy. Near zero means weak ranking on this sample.

Source/mode       rho rank   phi rank   rho within-10s   rho motion-adjusted
GLIM OFF           0.121      0.107       0.067           0.074
GLIM ON            0.125      0.187      -0.082          -0.051
Kimera OFF         0.718      0.123       0.374           0.542
Kimera ON          0.212      0.133       0.336           0.428

Within-10s pools centered, within-block percentile ranks. Motion-adjusted is
correlation of residual ranks after regressing out reference translation and
rotation magnitudes; it is descriptive, not a calibrated or causal model.
Five hundred resamples of contiguous 10 s blocks give the following descriptive
95% ranges for the GLOBAL rho ranking: GLIM OFF [-0.145,0.338], GLIM ON
[-0.099,0.363], Kimera OFF [0.493,0.837], Kimera ON [-0.024,0.479]. There are
only 12 time blocks. No independent-edge p-value/generalization claim is made.

Key qualification: Kimera ON has a much weaker GLOBAL uncertainty ranking,
but it has not lost ALL local information. The within-block correlations
0.374 OFF / 0.336 ON are fairly similar; motion adjustment also retains a
positive ON relationship. Do not call Kimera ON covariance random/useless.

For each 3x3 marginal C=U diag(lambda) U^T, project discrepancy onto each

Kimera ON        4.15 / 4.27 / 3.18            18.40 / 20.68 / 19.56

The discrepancy/declared-uncertainty mismatch is not restricted to one tiny
eigenvalue or a single direction. Rotation is especially difficult to certify
because the reference attitude is not an exact independent truth signal.
No mixed-unit 6D principal direction is interpreted as a physical direction.

Full dense whitening preserves rotation-translation coupling. Median ratio
of dense e^T Sigma^-1 e to the sum of separate marginal channel energies:
GLIM OFF 1.050, GLIM ON 0.991, Kimera OFF 1.139, Kimera ON 1.032.
Individual ratios vary (Kimera OFF maximum 3.965). The calculation is a
diagnostic decomposition only; no block-diagonal factor was used or proposed.

All 141,491 input matrices are finite, symmetric and positive definite.
Maximum symmetry error: 0. Minimum eigenvalues: OFF 2.011844397e-10,
ON 2.167566471e-10. These mixed-unit minima are numerical-health checks, not
scalar confidence measures. There is no zero/negative covariance failure here.

5. Revision evolution: the strongest new lead
============================================
For the SAME 1,169 GLIM / 571 Kimera intervals that reach age 2 s in both modes,
compare their version at age 2 s with their latest available version at cutoff.
Last observed does not certify finalization, and its exact age varies.
Numbers below are medians of PER-INTERVAL ratios, not ratios of medians.

                   rho uncertainty ratio  rho discrepancy ratio  phi uncertainty ratio  phi discrepancy ratio
GLIM OFF                   1.0000                1.0000                 1.0000                1.0000
GLIM ON                    1.0000                0.9997                 1.0000                0.9999
Kimera OFF                 1.0136                0.9822                 0.9121                1.0006
Kimera ON                  0.5271                0.9460                 0.4898                0.9936

For Kimera ON, the corresponding median covariance-TRACE ratios are about
0.278 rho and 0.240 phi (square of the root-sum ratios, approximately 72%/76%
trace reductions). This is NOT a claim of 3.6x/4.2x uniform full-factor precision:
full matrices, directions, cross blocks and Jacobians can change too.

Kimera ON's discrepancy divided by reported root-sum uncertainty increases
by median factors 1.703 (rho) and 2.011 (phi) AFTER age 2 s. On 86.9% of the
571 intervals, rotational uncertainty decreases by at least 5% without even a
5% reduction in rotational discrepancy. The corresponding rho fraction is
43.4%. The 5% marker is merely descriptive material-change reporting, not a
gate, fit or new production parameter.

Earlier revisions are useful: first-to-age-2 Kimera ON median per-edge
discrepancy ratios are 0.557 rho and 0.623 phi. Mean improvements occur before
the extra late confidence gain. For first-to-cutoff, 79.5% of Kimera ON rho
intervals and 86.7% of phi intervals improve over their own first publication.
Do NOT revert to first-publication beliefs or disable stable-ID replacement.

The extra age-2-to-cutoff table is a descriptive refinement of the prespecified
first/1 s/2 s/cutoff audit, motivated by the initial age-wise result. It selects
no new estimator setting and uses no additional measurements or source runs.

7. Interpretation and boundary
===============================
Established here:
- Kimera OFF rho uncertainty has useful ranking information on this window.
- Its global ranking is weaker ON, but local/motion-adjusted information remains.
- GLIM trace and both sources' phi trace are weak rankings on this reference.
- Kimera ON continues gaining substantial declared confidence after most of
  its observed relative-mean improvement has occurred; GLIM does not show the
  same late pattern.
- No numerical covariance corruption or receiver duplication was found.

Not established:
- Exact true-error covariance, a valid universal scale, or complete cause of ON loss.
- Whether late confidence gain comes from genuinely informative native data,
  explicit CBS factors, information retained in a marginal prior, linearization,
  unmodelled errors, source/reference dependence, or a combination.
- Whether one change would generalize beyond this ETH1 window.

The source covariances are conditional graph-derived uncertainties. The classic
runbook notes different provenance: GLIM uses its direct smoother; Kimera LOCAL
removes tracked explicit CBS factors/anchors but does not certify removal of
past CBS information retained in a marginalization prior. Receiver stable-ID
replacement prevents stacking versions of one belief, but does not remove
cross-edge or cross-source shared information.

For discussion, the most focused next question is WHY Kimera ON's covariance
continues contracting after age 2 s with little further mean improvement.
Trace the actual covariance-graph/prior contributions for those SAME IDs if
authorized. This report does not initiate that investigation or propose
covariance clamping, freezing old covariances, Huber, health weighting, changing
lag, dropping revisions, or another estimator run.
