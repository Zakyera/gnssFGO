INTERPRETATION / NEXT DECISION
=============================
What is established: the first native relative factor alone produces the early
coupled state response; native visual data or a different IMU/PIM between OFF
and ON is not needed to explain this first A/B difference. The correct saved
native linear equations reproduce the ON response. Factor removal cannot
originate this first event, because no CBS removal has yet happened.

What is NOT established: whether GLIM's short-interval angular increment is
physically wrong, whether frame/timestamp/endpoint mean alignment is wrong,
whether native graph sensitivity/model interaction is excessive, or whether a
specific implementation bug causes the later oscillations. A low full-path GLIM
ATE alone does not validate every0.2s relative rotation. Neither does a native
solver faithfully solving its equations prove the measurements are semantically
correct. Low covariance calibration is not assumed or investigated here.

Recommended next step, NOT launched: trace this ONE x4->x5 angular disagreement
against the shared raw CPT7 gyro over the exact endpoints, retaining source and
receiver time/frame/bias conventions and auditing the endpoint-alignment chain.
Determine whether the disagreement exists at GLIM's native source mean or is
introduced by transport/alignment. Keep covariance/weights and native smart
factors unchanged. This is more specific than another blind full rerun or prior
adjustment. Do not silently promote a factor-dropping fix from this experiment.
