CONCLUSION
==========
We found objective/linearization mismatches in the actual saved ON graphs, but
have NOT established the root cause of the large CBS-ON instability.

The native CBS relative-pose factor uses an approximate Jacobian: it computes
the derivative of Between(Xi,Xj) but omits the subsequent Local/Log chain rule.
All95 saved relative-pose factors fail at least one declared cost-derivative
check. The complete chain rule agrees with finite differences. This is a
confirmed approximation in the deployed equations, NOT proof of a wrong belief
frame, timestamp, measurement, or corrupted graph.

Its overall gradient discrepancy is modest,0.137--0.150% in these five windows.
That is too small by itself to declare it the cause of a~10deg window tilt.
Weakly constrained coupled directions could amplify an equation difference,
but that causal sensitivity has NOT been tested here. Avoid headlines saying
“CBS bug found/fixed” or “GTSAM is broken”. The fast approximation is explicit
in the library source and is not evidence of a new accidental regression.

The initial pose prior also fails the exact derivative check; the implementation
returns an identity Jacobian. Native smart-stereo cost/Schur-gradient disagreement
remains, as already observed in CP-V36. IMU, bias-between and other initial-prior
checks pass. Consequently we cannot honestly use the native linear gradient
as a certificate that the reported nonlinear objective has converged.

One additional full native GN step reduces reported total cost in4/5 windows
but does not reverse the previous window tilt in any of the five. At update24
it repairs much of the remaining IMU inconsistency while increasing net tilt
9.51->9.81deg. Thus “just run more iterations” is not demonstrated to repair
the shaking. This one-step result does NOT prove that a properly converged
solution would remain tilted or that all extra iterations are useless.

DECISION / NEXT PROPOSAL (NOT EXECUTED)
======================================
All five fail native nonlinear-convergence admission. Per preregistered
protocol, zero iterative optimizations were launched. No stopping-gradient
threshold was relaxed and no explicit-landmark model substituted.

The most focused next causal test is a saved-checkpoint equation ablation:
correct ONLY the CBS relative-pose Jacobian chain rule, with its residual,
measurements, priors, noise and native smart factors unchanged. Compare the
same one-step response/current native equations first; quantify whether the
small derivative difference materially changes the tilt/bias direction. The
pose-prior and visual caveats must remain explicit, not silently repaired in
the same ablation. A tiny effect would demote this hypothesis; a large effect
would justify focused safety checks, not an immediate full sequence.
