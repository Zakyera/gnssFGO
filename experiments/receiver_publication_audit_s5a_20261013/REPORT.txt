S5a — Why the ETH1 WNOA receiver exceeded its live budget
2026-10-13. Retained-data and code audit only.

CONCLUSION

The shared-native GLIM covariance path's observed benefit remains intact.
The recorded receiver stop is explained by growing global publication work,
amplified by a late increase in nonlinear iterations and numerical recovery.
It is not explained by a late explosion in source revision arrival rate or a
failed cache. No new estimator correctness bug is established by this audit.

The important distinction: full-history iSAM2 does not inherently require a
fresh whole-graph nonlinear certificate before every output. Our private strict
publication wrapper does that, including repeated full-current-Values gradient
checks. Its cost grows with history and with the number of refinement passes.
Those checks protect previously established numerical correctness; removing
them or relaxing their tolerance is not an authorized engineering fix.

The next narrowly scoped proposal is E3a, starting with the WNOA motion factor's
velocity/angular-rate derivative blocks: reuse the authors' existing closed-
form blocks while retaining the exact current residual and numerical pose
blocks. First validate and benchmark the factor in isolation. Do not change
the solver policy, covariance, source timing or model. This audit does not
establish that this patch alone would make a 160 s live run pass.

7. WHAT THIS AUDIT DOES NOT ESTABLISH

No separate timer for factor differentiation versus QR elimination, copying,
gradient accumulation or IO exists in this capture. Therefore we cannot state
that a particular family accounts for X% of receiver wall time, or promise a
particular speedup from analytic derivatives. There is no evidence here of
receiver marginalization cost, because it does not marginalize, nor a GLIM
provider regression. The cache has no recorded failure, but this is not a new
complete correctness proof. S5's wire/lifecycle/replay gates remain incomplete.

No new ATE conclusion, diagnosis of belief accuracy, covariance calibration,
or explanation of why this trajectory needs additional nonlinear iterations
is drawn from a timing log. CPU frequency/affinity contention was not newly
controlled; the audit is observational.

8. ONE NEXT STEP, NOT A NEW MODEL

Recommend the first small part of E3a, not another selective-refinement policy
(the earlier E4 trial is already rejected) and not an immediate fixed-lag change.

Create an isolated default-off WNOA motion-factor wrapper. Reuse the authors'
four analytic V/W Jacobian blocks; keep the existing evaluateError_ residual,
two numerical Pose3 blocks, whitening, Qc, covariance, event order, source
factor revisions and strict publication rule unchanged. At fixed poses,
e=[xi-dt*u_i;Jr(xi)^-1*u_j-u_i] is affine in the endpoint velocities/rates;
their derivatives need no finite-difference perturbations.

Before adoption: independent numerical derivative checks over nonzero rotations,
world velocities/body rates and dense noise; exact residual and tight full
Gaussian-block comparison; factor-level benchmark on representative retained
states. This first check needs no new sensor experiment or changed solver.
Only after it passes should a separate authorized saved-journal equivalence/
timing test retain the original state/cost/stationarity/rollback gates.
Do not blindly switch useAutoDiff=false for every block, advertise a live
speedup from call counts, or promote the candidate if numerical gates fail.

No E3a implementation or estimator execution occurred in S5a. Stop here.
