2. What was tested

BoundedAdaptive.h delegates the nonlinear step to the installed GTSAM optimizer:
LevenbergMarquardtParams::CeresDefaults(), MULTIFRONTAL_QR,
lambdaInitial1e-4, lambdaFactor2, lambdaLowerBound1e-16,
lambdaUpperBound1e32, minModelFidelity1e-3, diagonalDamping=true,
minDiagonal1e-6, maxDiagonal1e32, useFixedLambdaFactor=false.
No policy or parameter was selected after observing the real fixture result.

The temporary step solves (schematically):
  (J'J + lambda D) delta = -J'r,
where D is the clamped Hessian diagonal. Native tryLambda() evaluates actual
versus predicted objective decrease and adjusts lambda. The wrapper only drives
the public linearize()/tryLambda() interface and counts EVERY attempted solve,
including rejected attempts. Rejected steps reuse the same solve linearization;
accepted steps cause fresh linearization. Extra gradient evaluations are audit
work, included in the reported runtime but not additional linear solves.

Cost stopping tolerances are zero in this private diagnostic, so a small cost
change cannot substitute for the unchanged normalized-gradient <=1e-5 gate.
Budget is20 total attempts, NOT20 accepted steps plus uncounted retries.
The existing fl2a::CheckedGradient function is used unchanged.

Damping is only in the temporary linear system. It never becomes a measurement
factor, covariance rescaling, physical prior, or exported marginal information.
The candidate returns diagnostic Values, never commits a caller's state.
