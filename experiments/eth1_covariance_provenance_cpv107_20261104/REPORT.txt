BOTTOM LINE
The concern is supported, especially for late Kimera revisions consumed by
fusion. The strongest new evidence is a systematic, direction-dependent late
contraction already present in native Alpha coordinates, not a conversion or
transport scaling error. The persistent mixed-prior information route exists
in the actual implementation. We cannot yet assign a percentage of CP103's
covariance reduction to that route rather than state-dependent native visual
information or other covariance-model approximations.

Do not equate every covariance reduction with a bug: new camera/IMU information
normally changes uncertainty as a belief matures. Here the problematic signature
is continuing confidence growth after reference discrepancy has stopped improving.
CP106 independently demonstrated that this covariance schedule materially affects
fusion ATE in the fixed saved ON stream. Its freeze was diagnostic, not a fix.

3. WHEN THE CONTRACTION OCCURS
Both modes first retire a native pose at update26 /8.499951453s. Every subsequent
source update retires a pose (1467OFF/1466ON updates). Consequently, associating
a covariance drop with a retirement update is not a causal test: all routine
new-measurement/relinearization updates are in that same category after warm-up.

The strongest single-step decrease usually occurs early (median source age0.4s
in both modes); it is not generally one final catastrophic covariance collapse.
The important difference is the continuing late contraction. In the last0.4s
before each edge's LAST SAVED ACCEPTED VERSION, native translation trace divided
by the preceding version has median:
  OFF0.996299 (0.37% decrease per revision), n4322 transitions;
  ON 0.835611 (16.44% decrease per revision), n4316 transitions.
Only0.21% of these ON transitions increase variance, versus23.32%OFF. None has
a single-step drop greater than50%. This is sustained late tightening, not just
a few extreme outliers. Source updates are normally~0.2s but gaps remain; do not
interpret these as constant-time derivative estimates or independent samples.

The displayed source-age curve retains eligible rows within0.25s of each age
cutoff, so n varies slightly by cutoff. The matrix/reference comparisons above
retain the identical1454 cohort and are not based on that varying curve.

  :3736 onward also queries this route for older revised beliefs.

Pinned cbs/src/bpsam/bpsam.cpp:4737–4865 constructs LOCAL by copying MAIN's factors,
excluding tracked explicit CBS slots and anchor beliefs, and evaluating at MAIN
states. :3091–3203 tracks original factor slot/pointer/endpoints; once a factor

There is no operation here decomposing that marginal prior into separate native
and peer histories. CP-K9's original-chart preservation keeps the prior function;
it does not remove peer information from it. CP102's numerical correction is
therefore not a provenance correction.

Existing CP67 native BPSAM fixture demonstrated the route: LOCAL equals its
native-only control before absorption, but differs after absorbing a peer into
a marginal prior. That was an older one-boundary diagnostic, not a CP103 snapshot
or a numerical measure of this run's confidence excess. Current CP76 persistent
lifecycle uses the same explicit-filter limitation, and CP103 explicitly marks
native-only outgoing information FALSE. The route is established; the amount
of information recirculated in each CP103 matrix is not recorded.

There is also a separate route: native visual information depends on states and
validity decisions influenced by CBS. CP98's existing fixed-measurement test
changed endpoint visual contributors16->66 and visual Hessian trace2.63x solely
by changing evaluated states. CP97 observed covariance divergence before first
marginalization. Removing explicit peer factors does not remove these effects.
Those tests concern earlier captures and do not prove CP103's complete cause.

GLIM's configured shared_native/native_local provider has separately maintained
native Gaussian history and positive native admission (CP82 qualified route),
unlike Kimera's copy-MAIN-and-filter extraction. Thus both being persistent does
not mean they export identically sourced uncertainty. Even native Gaussian
history does not certify independent/calibrated noise at CBS-influenced states.

6. WHAT FUSION DOES AND DOES NOT DOUBLE COUNT
Receiver.h:211–234 constructs a full-covariance WnoaRelativeFactor and replaces
the preceding factor slot for the same stable ID. CP106 proved identical event
accounting and exact baseline replay. There is no evidence of simply appending
every revision as an independent new factor. Revisions after absorption are
sealed/expired according to the existing rule, not stacked into the prior.

However, different source/interval factors enter without their cross-source or
cross-interval covariance. If their beliefs reuse information through overlapping
source windows or CBS feedback, per-belief6x6 matrices alone cannot represent
that joint dependence. Two distinct issues must be distinguished:
  a) source covariance becoming too small relative to reference discrepancy;
  b) correlated exported beliefs being combined without their joint covariance.
Replacing one ID correctly solves neither source provenance nor inter-ID dependence.

CP106 held all received means, arrivals/order, GLIM matrices and settings fixed,
changing only later Kimera matrices to their first accepted matrices. ONLINE ATE
fell0.094335->0.052415m; separate7s-finalized0.091336->0.043808m on the identical
mask. This establishes conditional covariance-schedule influence on ATE, not
the exact source mechanism. Short-scale RPE did not improve; ONLINE path inflation
remains. Do not promote freezing or a fitted multiplier as a general solution.

7. NEXT DISCRIMINATING TEST — PROPOSAL ONLY, NOT RUN
First qualify a bounded Kimera diagnostic with two marginal-information histories
from initialization: existing MAIN/mixed and positively admitted native-only.
Use existing saved native fixtures where possible; do not merely filter the
current mixed prior, reset it, or delete it. At fixed measurements and the SAME
linearization states/active keys, compare old/revised relative covariance before
and after selected absorption boundaries. This isolates the explicit absorbed
peer contribution from the separate changed-state/visual-validity effect.

If the preserved checkpoints cannot supply the required native histories, a
short separately authorized passive capture is needed before that counterfactual;
the ordinary CP103 logs do not contain complete historical Gaussian graphs.
Require no-peer equality, known-peer positive control, conservation, full6x6
frame parity and rollback checks before any receiver or live deployment test.
Then evaluate a provenance-correct covariance export with the means unchanged
in a saved receiver replay; this is a future authorization, not work done here.
Retain native CBS corrections while separating uncertainty provenance; simply
switching all factors to temporary changes another part of the experiment and
does not by itself validate uncertainty or remove all dependence.
