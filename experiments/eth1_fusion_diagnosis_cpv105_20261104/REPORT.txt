CP-V105 — why does the latest fusion result worsen?
Saved-data diagnosis, 2026-11-04. No capture or receiver replay was run.

CONCLUSION
There is a concrete confidence-versus-error mismatch in the revised Kimera
beliefs actually consumed by fusion. It is a strong candidate mechanism, not
yet a demonstrated cause of the trajectory regression. Initial belief
covariances alone miss this effect. No production correction is qualified.

There are two distinguishable issues:
1. ONLINE publication has short-scale motion/orientation fluctuations and a
   longer estimated polyline. Fixed-lag finalization substantially reduces it.
2. The ON accuracy degradation persists after finalization, so publication
   jitter alone cannot explain it. The weighting of revised, correlated
   relative beliefs is the leading hypothesis for the persistent component.

WHY THIS COULD AFFECT FUSION
The pinned Receiver.h dispatch uses the supplied full covariance directly in
noiseModel::Gaussian::Covariance, replacing the previous factor for a revision.
It does not simply accumulate every revision as another factor. However,
distinct source/overlapping relative-belief factors do not carry joint
cross-factor covariance. Both persistent sources carry peer history, so
different IMUs do not make their beliefs independent. Increasing confidence
in revised correlated information can give that information too much influence.
Full matrix directions, geometry, overlapping intervals and marginalization
matter; trace ratios alone are NOT exact factor-weight or influence ratios.
