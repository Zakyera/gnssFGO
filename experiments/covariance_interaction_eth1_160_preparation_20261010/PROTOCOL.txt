Purpose
-------
Understand two different levels before changing any weights:
  1. GLIM -> Kimera and Kimera -> GLIM: incoming covariance and relative mean,
     receiver's native constraints, actual factor insertion and retirement,
     then evolution of the outgoing belief covariance.
  2. GLIM + Kimera -> gnssFGO: separate their relative-factor information from
     the WNOA motion prior at the SAME receiver state/linearization point.

Keep all receivers Gaussian at scale 1. No tuning, floors, covariance bridges,
health weighting, robust losses or accuracy-selected sampling in this task.
The central receiver has NO IMU measurement factors. Native sources still use
their IMUs. WNOA, not WNOJ, is the central motion model. Initial priors are an
additional factor family and must not be hidden inside WNOA or a source.

Capture A: every belief, minimal continuous logging
-------------------------------------------------
Keep wire bag, native logs, revisions, events, receipts and slot lifetimes.
For each layer distinguish:
 - sender/session/BeliefID and revision; source and receiver endpoint IDs/times;
 - publication, observed availability, insertion/update and retirement times;
 - raw mean, temporally adapted mean, full source covariance, inserted covariance;
 - extraction graph (GLIM direct vs Kimera LOCAL), rank/jitter/fallback status;
 - available active-pose window and whether the belief is still jointly valid.

Do not confuse source classic wire publications with the downstream rolling
smoother revision stream. They need not have the same number of messages or
covariance payload at the same wall time. Join exact identities AND versions.
One revised belief replaces one factor, not another independent observation.

Covariance summaries, separately per stream/layer/direction and 10 s time bin:
 - trace(Sigma_phi_phi) [rad^2] and trace(Sigma_rho_rho) [m^2];
 - per-axis standard deviations and 3x3 marginal eigenvalues;
 - minimum/full spectrum as numerical health only, symmetry and finite checks;
 - full rotation/rho cross block and dimensionless block-normalized coupling;
 - first, fixed physical ages 1/2/3/4 s, last jointly valid versions per ID;
 - contraction ratios on matched IDs/endpoints with censored tails separate.
Never average every revision as an independent belief or mix rad^2 with m^2
in a single confidence score. rho is the existing SE(3) log coordinate, not
automatically Cartesian position error. Retain full 6x6 covariance in whitening.

Compare OFF/ON on identical physical relative intervals and matched revision
age, not source row number or nearest unrelated edge. Record unmatched counts.
Do not pick only low-covariance or high-error samples.

WNOA covariance is 12D and includes pose/rate units and time powers; it cannot
be ranked against a source's6D covariance trace. Directional quantities must
use the same variables, coordinates and scaling. If comparing Hessian spectra,
use one common normalization D from Htotal for ALL families, not a different
normalization per source. Information curvature is resistance to motion;
gradient is directional pull. Neither alone equals trajectory accuracy.
At a converged solution family gradients can be large and cancel; the sum
being near zero is not evidence that no factor matters. A low final residual
can indicate the graph followed a strong bad factor, not that it was correct.
