FULL GRANDTOUR COVARIANCE RANKING — COMPLETED 2026-10-23

Scope and outcome
-----------------
Two diagnostics on the saved full ETH1 CP-K23, EIG-2 CP-K24/24A and SPX-3
CP-K26 captures: uncertainty-versus-relative-error curves, and error when
retaining the lowest-uncertainty fraction. Translation and rotation are
separate, at first publication and near four seconds of source revision age.
Eight six-panel overview figures, each in PNG and SVG, are in outputs/.

No source, fuser, replay or container was run. No covariance, model, launch,
weight, guard, baseline result or previous analysis artifact was changed.
No fitted multiplier, factor rejection or tuning recommendation is installed.

Main conclusion: covariance has useful ranking information, particularly
Kimera translation at first publication. It is NOT uniformly calibrated or
a reliable universal health gate. Ranking quality depends on source, sequence,
translation versus rotation, and revision age. Constant scaling can change
overall source influence, but cannot repair an incorrectly ranked belief.

These are local relative-belief errors, NOT new full-trajectory ATE results.
The existing native GLIM/native Kimera/ONLINE fusion reports remain the
trajectory comparison. A lower ratio on a selection curve does not establish
that deleting those factors would improve fusion: topology/information change.

1. Reading the figures
---------------------
*_reliability.png: x is sqrt(mean block covariance trace) in each approximately
equal-count uncertainty bin; y is actual tangent-error norm RMS for that bin.
Each point pools beliefs with similar reported uncertainty. Above the grey
diagonal means reference error is larger than that reported norm scale.
This is NOT a test against independent, perfect ground truth, a calibrated
confidence bound, or a full-matrix NEES/NIS calculation.

*_risk.png: rank beliefs by increasing block covariance trace, then retain
10%,15%,...,100%, including ties. y = selected RMS / all-belief RMS for the
same source/mode/age cohort. Below 1 means selection improved average error;
above 1 means it selected a worse subset. The full RMS appears in the legend.
OFF and ON have separate denominators: compare legend values for absolute
accuracy, not curve height alone. No beliefs were removed from any estimator.

All figures: blue OFF, orange ON; rows ETH1/EIG-2/SPX-3, columns GLIM/Kimera.
First publication describes information when first sent. Near4s is a diagnostic
snapshot of later revisions, not an instruction to delay online operation.

4. Important exception: confident revised SPX-3 startup beliefs
--------------------------------------------------------------
Kimera SPX-3 ON near4s: all 875 beliefs RMS 4.518 mm; lowest-uncertainty half
(438 beliefs) RMS 5.075 mm, 12.3% WORSE. Spearman correlation remains positive
at 0.604: a good typical ranking can coexist with a harmful confident tail.

Inspection of the SAME retained samples locates much of this to three initial
edges; they account for 54.65% of this cohort's total squared translation error.
They remain included in all plots; no startup trimming or rescoring was done.

This is a localization of the diagnostic exception, NOT a proven explanation

age weighting or multiplier follows automatically from this observation.

5. Rotation must be considered separately
-----------------------------------------
The same confident-half test for rotation behaves differently:
- ETH1 Kimera ON: 1.102 first, 1.172 near4s (10.2% and 17.2% worse subsets).
- EIG-2 GLIM ON: 1.001 first, 1.007 near4s (essentially no selection benefit).
- SPX-3 Kimera ON: 0.638 first, 0.654 near4s (useful selection).
Do not infer rotational reliability from a successful translation plot or
combine m^2 and rad^2 into one trace or proposed global tuning coefficient.

Magnitude also differs substantially. Across the matched cohorts, actual
rotation-error RMS / sqrt(mean rotation trace) ranges about 5.3--51.9;
translation ranges about 1.05--16.3. These are discrepancy ratios, NOT fitted
covariance multipliers or statistical consistency certificates. Squaring or
copying them into the optimizer would not be justified by this diagnostic.

6. What this supports next — no action performed
-----------------------------------------------
Separate two questions:
(a) calibration: how much overall trust does a source/block deserve?
(b) ranking: does its reported uncertainty identify its worse intervals?
A fixed positive covariance multiplier can change (a), never (b). The curves
are unchanged if all covariances of one source/block are multiplied by a
constant. Therefore a simple scale may be a useful controlled sensitivity
experiment, but it cannot fix the confident-startup exception or weak ranking.

Do not choose a universal ON/OFF scale, trace threshold or drop-half policy
from these plots. First-versus-revised behavior and translation-versus-rotation
must remain explicit. If a manual scaling experiment is later authorized,
predeclare a small common candidate set and judge it on held-out sequences,
with untouched source runs/means/topology; do not optimize each mission's ATE.
No specific scale has been selected, and no follow-up run has been launched.

7. Limits and retained provenance
--------------------------------
Reference is IE-TC, sharing CPT7 with the sources, not independent prism truth.
Its covariance/cross-correlation with the source is unavailable; reference,
timing/calibration errors and estimator bias can contribute to discrepancies.
Trace removes directional/cross-term information; a block trace alone does
not tell us optimizer information or correction influence. Overlapping edges
and revisions are dependent; no independence-based significance tests are used.
Low uncertainty may also select slower/easier motion, not detect health causally.
LOCAL covariance paired with corrected MAIN means is the frozen protocol,
not proof that the payload is statistically calibrated or independent.

These samples are source versions from incoming journals, not a certificate
that every version was installed in the receiver. ETH1 known expired revisions
and EIG-2/SPX-3 acknowledged finite-recording EOF omissions remain unchanged.
Peer mean-only covariance alignment is unchanged and not audited here; the
relative reference uses each original source belief's own physical endpoints.
