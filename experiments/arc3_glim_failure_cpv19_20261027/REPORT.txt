BOTTOM LINE
-----------
Two failures must be distinguished:
1. GLIM's actual estimated motion deteriorated badly during the recorded lidar
   outages. Kimera stayed locally much healthier, but the current CBS temporal
   matching could not attach its short relative-motion beliefs to the sparse
   GLIM states. Existing temporary CBS factors subsequently expired.
2. At 513.567510711s of the admitted window, the added LOCAL covariance provider
   failed its extraction/chart consistency guard. It invalidated itself, and
   the experiment controller stopped the run. This was not a logged CUDA/MAIN
   solver crash, but neither was it merely a harmless guard on a healthy path.

Thus the concrete rescue limitation is temporal support during missing scans,
not evidence that Kimera had no useful information or that GPU batching broke.
The precise floating-point branch inside the final covariance guard was not
recorded and cannot be certified from these artifacts alone.

WHY KIMERA DID NOT RESCUE GLIM THROUGH THE GAP
--------------------------------------------
The frozen source bridge attaches a Kimera relative factor to TWO existing
GLIM pose states. It does not create intermediate GLIM states or interpolate
onto an independent source support grid. Saved resolved parameters require:
  endpoint tolerance0.12s; interval mismatch<=0.06s; duration ratio0.5..2;
  duration_aware_edge; pending retry age5s.
Kimera's usual0.20s edge cannot simply be attached across a1.5--1.9s GLIM gap:
that would constrain the wrong physical interval.

  lie in501.4--512.1s finds52unmatchable even using ALL saved GLIM timestamps.

  returning g5014->5015 states and inserted at512.272269s. This audit is not

  A0.20s Kimera edge near507.17s matches two GLIM timestamps but they span

This is an architectural limitation of this source-to-source temporal policy
under missing lidar states. It is distinct from the central fusion receiver,
which DOES have independent100ms support states and continues consuming Kimera.
Relaxing timestamps/duration checks would not be a mathematically valid repair.

IMPLICATION / NEXT STEP (NOT EXECUTED)
------------------------------------
Do not launch another full run unchanged and do not simply disable the guard.
Two separately scoped issues need treatment:
1. Specify and test a valid gap-support mechanism so a healthy camera source
   can constrain a lidar estimator without suitably timed scan states. Options
   require deliberate design (e.g. inertial intermediate support states plus
   correctly matched factors); arbitrary snapping/composing correlated beliefs
   with naive covariance addition is not an acceptable shortcut.
2. Isolate the covariance calculation and record the failed joint matrix,
   endpoint/chart inputs, both outputs, jitter flags, norms and conditioning.
   A bounded diagnostic reproduction is needed for an exact numerical repair;
   it is NOT authorized or launched by this report. Preserve LOCAL provenance
   and test the equations before changing an extraction method or tolerance.
The dataset now provides evidence for an important limitation: a healthy peer
alone is insufficient if the recipient has no compatible state-time support.
