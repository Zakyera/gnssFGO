FL4z — GLIM/Kimera uncertainty versus relative-belief quality
Saved ETH1, SPX-3 and EIG-2 OFF/ON captures | 2026-10-17
=================================================================

Result
------
The covariances contain SOME quality information, especially Kimera's
translation block. They are NOT reliable, universally calibrated measures
of relative-belief accuracy, and a low trace does not reliably identify the
better sender. There is no universal OFF-to-ON covariance-shrinkage factor.
No covariance, injection policy, estimator, fusion model or run was changed.

The clearest failure of a simple confidence rule is EIG-2 GLIM: its initial
translation uncertainty is almost unchanged between lower- and higher-error
intervals, and its uncertainty is SMALLER than on SPX-3 although its relative
translation error is much larger. This is already present in the raw payload;
it is not created solely by temporal mean alignment.

The useful positive finding is Kimera's translation covariance: higher-error
intervals usually have larger reported variance. This ranking signal is not
the same as calibrated magnitude, nor does it by itself tell the receiver
which of two disagreeing estimators is correct.

2. Coordinates, units and interpretation
---------------------------------------
Both sources report CPT7-body relative Pose3 beliefs with [phi,rho] tangent
ordering. Use the actual dense covariance and e=Log(Z^-1 Z_reference), the
BetweenFactor residual convention used by the extraction machinery.
Rotation and translation block traces are kept separate (rad^2 and m^2).
sqrt(trace(block)) is an aggregate RMS-norm uncertainty scale, NOT a per-axis
standard deviation, confidence radius or95% error bound.

Translation error columns below are Cartesian relative-translation error
norms. Covariance/error correlation and normalization use tangent rho;
Cartesian and rho norms are retained separately, not silently conflated.
Full6D normalization includes cross blocks. The3D diagnostics use genuine
marginal blocks, not conditional covariance or a Schur complement.

Reference: dataset IE-TC /boxi/inertial_explorer/tc/odometry, cpt7_imu body,
translation interpolation plus shortest-path SO3 interpolation, exact logged
endpoints. No global SE3 fitting, no prism lever arm, no clock adjustment.
This reference SHARES CPT7 and is not independent noiseless ground truth.
Reference uncertainty, temporal correlations and source/reference cross
correlations are unknown. Consequently covariance-normalized reference errors
are NOT innovation NIS or a rigorous true-error NEES/calibration test.
They reveal discrepancy with the retained reference, not its unique cause.

complete matrix. Nominal95% thresholds are7.8147 (3D),12.5916 (6D), for a
correct zero-mean Gaussian error against exact truth. Here reference is NOT
exact/independent, so fractions below these thresholds are descriptive only.

This is strong evidence that treating the retained covariance as a calibrated
standalone error distribution against IE-TC is not justified. It does not
prove that all discrepancy comes from estimator overconfidence; systematic
reference/frame/time/mean errors and unknown reference uncertainty can matter.
In particular the rotational discrepancy is common to both sources and needs
care before anyone fits a rotation scale. This audit applies no scale.

Trace also hides directionality and cross blocks. First-belief median3x3
rotation eigenvalue ratios range~2.1..17.8; translation~1.8..10.2. Full6D
normalization differs from adding separately normalized marginal blocks.
Graph influence is J^T Sigma^-1 J plus topology, current residuals and other
factors, not1/trace. We did not rerun optimization or claim exact influence
percentages for GLIM, Kimera or WNOA from these tables.

8. What this supports, and what it does not
------------------------------------------
Supported: uncertainty-aware sharing is a worthwhile target, but the existing
trace cannot yet decide 'inject / do not inject' reliably across sequences.
Kimera's translational covariance is not devoid of information. GLIM's weak
quality discrimination on ETH1/EIG-2 and the absolute reference/covariance
mismatch must be understood separately from feedback or universal scaling.

Not supported: one global CBS-ON shrinkage correction, GT-derived per-sequence
gates, declaring either estimator always healthy, or treating lower covariance
as evidence a conflicting sender must be right. Shared IMU/data, successive
overlapping factors and previous CBS effects also violate independence
assumptions; temporary retirement is not a proof that all correlation vanished.

Before an injection policy, the bounded next diagnostic would distinguish
local Gaussian geometric/inertial precision from unmodeled mean error, and
check whether native quality signals add information beyond covariance.
That is a proposal only. No weighting, gating or follow-up run is implemented.
