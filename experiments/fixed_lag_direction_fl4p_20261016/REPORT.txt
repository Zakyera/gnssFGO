FL4p — first harmful baseline step: direction/model audit
2026-10-16. Diagnostic complete; no production repair or accuracy claim.

CENTRAL FINDING
At unchanged Kimera update89, the proposed direction is initially downhill,
but the full native step overshoots. Its cached quadratic substantially
misrepresents the slope at the current estimate. 99.93% of the signed total
slope discrepancy is in native Kimera IMU factors; one old boundary IMU factor
accounts for98.63%. Fresh current-point IMU Jacobians agree with independent
finite differences. This identifies a specific local-model failure at this
update, not corrupt cache assembly, a wrong GLIM payload, or a demonstrated
IMU Jacobian implementation defect.

The concrete mechanism is that marginalization fixes old linearization points
(NOT the estimated poses). The states can move away from these anchors, but
forceFullSolve still excludes fixed keys from relinearization. A correct solve
of equations at those old anchors can therefore propose a bad nonlinear step.
We have NOT proved that this one mechanism explains every later failure or
that a particular replacement solver is production-ready.

METHOD
At the same post-removal graph define d0=Local(theta,X_before), d1=native delta,
s=d1-d0, and X(alpha)=Retract(theta,d0+alpha*s). Evaluate independent cloned
factors at alpha0, ±1e-2 through ±1e-8, .25, .5,1. The unanchored marginal
prior is evaluated as its saved Gaussian in its owning theta chart, including
the constant; ordinary LinearContainerFactor::error would omit that prior.

Cached model slope is (H*d0-g)^T*s. Independently linearize native factors at
X_before, transport the path tangent into that local chart, and preserve the
prior's original chart using FL4l's diagnostic OriginalChartPrior. Compare
the resulting directional derivative against centered finite differences.
This diagnostic current-point graph is NEVER installed in the smoother.

Repeat objective probes with clone triangulation caches explicitly reset,

DECISION / LIMIT
First bad baseline step is an oversized downhill step built from a misleading
old-anchor model, dominated by a boundary native IMU factor. This is stronger
localization than "covariance too small" or "iSAM2 is broken"; it does not
establish that marginalization itself is wrong or that all subsequent failures
have the same cause. FL4o update108 occurred on an already changed trajectory
and was deliberately not investigated in this task.

The justified next candidate is a current-estimate local solve with the
existing marginal prior evaluated/differentiated in its ORIGINAL coordinates,
plus objective-based step acceptance. Do not unfreeze/reanchor priors naively,
change covariance, inherit removed CBS information, or infer that more solves
of the unchanged old-anchor equations repair the issue. No candidate is
implemented or authorized by this report. Stop for discussion.
