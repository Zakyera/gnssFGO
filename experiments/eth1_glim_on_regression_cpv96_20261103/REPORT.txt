CP-V96 — CP91 versus CP95 GLIM ON regression: saved-data diagnosis
2026-11-03. ANALYSIS COMPLETE; ROOT CAUSE OF ATE CHANGE NOT PROVEN.

Bottom line
===========
The higher ATE is real on identical evaluation support, not a mask/configuration
mix-up. It is principally a different broad trajectory shape, not increased
short-distance jitter. There is also a concrete, previously hidden difference:
some early Kimera->GLIM covariance matrices are substantially smaller in CP95,
despite almost unchanged whole-run covariance medians. This is a lead for
causal testing, NOT proof that these factors caused the ATE regression.

No source capture, receiver replay, optimizer execution, build, tuning, deletion,
parameter change or terminal-evidence modification took place. All results here
are supplemental. Old CP91 and new CP95 terminal output hashes (221/426) pass.

Observed divergence chronology
==============================
At native state1, .919669590s, GLIM differs2.367576mm/.002886deg, before any CBS.
Initial pose is identical. Across30 pre-insertion poses, maximum difference
is3.681792mm. First K2G insertion is update30 (state3.819741688s); its incoming
raw mean and full covariance are EXACTLY identical between runs. Its native-IMU
time-aligned mean differs because the receiving trajectory already differs.
The first applied G2K interval g29->g31 (end3.919748651s, applied at Kimera update4)
already has different means/covariance. First K2G covariance difference is
k3->k4 at4.099975s, ratio .989057. First ratio<.5 is5.499967s. Large early
covariance changes therefore precede the first changed GLIM binding at8.299952s.
Endpoint mismatch alone cannot explain the beginning of covariance divergence.

Fifteen K2G endpoint bindings differ, eleven before30s, last at124.099291s.
Native duration_aware_edge matching prioritizes duration error, then endpoint
error, among currently available states within .12s. That is a real execution
difference, but neither its cause nor its contribution to total ATE is proven.
G2K endpoint pairs match; five application update headers differ.

Historical reproducibility evidence, not a new causal proof
=========================================================
CP-K16e REPORT_K16E.txt independently demonstrated scheduling-dependent GLIM
random-grid sampling on identical raw scan/RNG reset: two selected sets in six
two-thread calls, one set in six one-thread calls. Current saved profiles still
enable random-grid sampling with two threads. Current source retains worker RNG
and guided OpenMP scheduling. CP91/95 did not save equivalent prepared-cloud
snapshots at their first divergence, so that specific mechanism cannot be
certified as the cause or magnitude of THIS regression. Do not call it harmless.
Fusion is downstream only; changed host scheduling is a hypothesis, not a
feedback connection or established explanation. No thread setting was changed.

Conclusion / next bounded check
==============================
The evidence supports a different closed-loop numerical history, including
different early source-produced belief uncertainties and some different timing
associations. It does NOT identify a new frame bug, insertion covariance scaling
bug, or prove a systematic CBS degradation beyond these saved runs.
The most useful next causal question is why Kimera's outgoing covariance differs
so much over5–10s despite identical OFF Kimera output, and whether those early
weights alter GLIM's later shape. Compare native Kimera information/visual support
and retained-prior state at the first divergent covariance, then a frozen-input
single-variable checkpoint test if the needed states can be captured. Hold native
factors, means, endpoint IDs and application schedule fixed when testing covariance.
Do NOT start by tuning weights or repeating a full campaign. No such capture or
optimizer test was launched or authorized by this saved-only investigation.

Limitations
===========
Two ON realizations do not estimate a repeatability distribution. Median trace
stability does not establish per-event stability, calibration or independence.
IE-TC shares CPT7 and is not independent prism GT. Separate IMUs do not eliminate
persistent peer-feedback correlation. Logged incremental residuals are not a
nonlinear stationarity certificate. ATE changes and covariance changes do not,
by themselves, establish causality. Estimated path length is not physical travel.
