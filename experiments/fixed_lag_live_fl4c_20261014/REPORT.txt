Scope and provenance
--------------------
This is one engineering pilot, not a CBS-OFF/ON accuracy comparison or a
production fixed-lag certification. Native GLIM and Kimera and the gnssFGO
receiver ran concurrently while the first 30 bag seconds of ETH1 mission
2024-10-01-11-29-55 were played at 0.10x, headless. The earlier FL4a and FL4b
failed preparations remain preserved; neither played bag data. FL4c is a fresh
run root and the sole completed source capture here.

The GLIM shared_native covariance provider requires its internal CBS mode
active_window_temporary. We selected that mode without enabling source-to-
source CBS reception: GLIM's effective receive flag was false, it had no
Kimera-belief subscription before playback, Kimera's receive flag was false,
and the source runner recorded zero G->K and K->G injections. Both still
published classic diagnostic beliefs for downstream fusion. This pairing was
checked at the resolved parameter, process/subscription and source-log levels;
it was not inferred from the mode name. A stale copied launch-profile path in
FL4b was caught before playback and corrected only in the new FL4c root.

Frozen receiver: independent 100 ms support clock after 59 IMU samples, 7 s
fixed lag, source-driven WNOA with Qc variances [.00375 x3,.015 x3], pose/V/W
priors 1e-5/10/3, no central IMU factor, no GNSS, full dense source covariance
at scale 1, Gaussian, no gating/health weighting, and stable-ID revision
replacement. The thin live-output wrapper and receiver executable are the
FL4a saved-prefix-checked build, SHA256
15a2ae458f5142c0b3341e9e444b25fc41f05a263cefbbb877c18c4a527718bc.
No estimator mathematics, source build, covariance or runtime policy changed
for this capture. The earlier FL3i strict whole-replay equivalence gate remains
FAILED; this pilot does not turn it into a pass.

before receiver initialization; 420+15,439+25=15,884. No unsupported end
events or pending factors remained. At EOF 319 inserted IDs had been absorbed
into fixed-lag marginalization and 101 were active; no old factor was revived.
All 15,884 event payloads were checked against committed records: covariance
entries exact and relative-mean log roundtrip max 1.94e-14. A protected
814-file input/build manifest rehashed unchanged.

Measured capacity at this operating point
-----------------------------------------
The complete service time accumulated per published 100 ms support was mean
39.06 ms, median 33.63 ms, p95 64.41 ms, maximum 104.26 ms. At 0.10x a
100 ms data interval supplies about 1 s wall time; this particular short run
met the predeclared 0.8 s p95 pilot target. Receipt-to-processing latency was
median 5.60 ms, p95 16.86 ms, max 32.71 ms. Monitored output age peaked at
0.100 s; 293/293 poses were published during source playback. Retained support
count was 71 and factor-slot high-water 175. The queue was fully drained.
These figures do not certify 1x playback, a long-run memory bound, a CBS-ON
run, or nonlinear stationarity of the fixed-lag estimate. The receiver itself
labels stationarity `not_checked_incremental_policy`.

Determinism and operational cleanup
-----------------------------------
A first journal-repeat command on the host could not load its Boost 1.74
runtime; it produced no receiver output. A second attempt under the default
sandbox could not access Docker; it also produced no receiver output. The
validated repeat was run once in the SAME Jammy receiver container used by
the live capture, with the same executable, journal, model arguments, runtime
libraries and CPU affinity. It finished in 11.014 s. All 16,365 archived
commit files, online/finalized state CSVs, both TUM trajectories and summary
were byte-identical. Those two preliminary environment failures are preserved
as separate logs and are not source or solver failures. No owned GLIM, Kimera,
receiver or ROS-master process remained at final inspection.

Decision and limits
-------------------
The bounded CBS-OFF live path is operational at 0.10x with complete source
accounting, timely publication and exact journal reproducibility. This does
not yet compare against ground truth, establish improvement, or validate a
matched CBS-ON run. GT metrics were deliberately not manufactured from a
partial prism overlap. Any matched ON capture requires a separate decision
and must preserve the same source profile, receiver binary and scoring mask.
