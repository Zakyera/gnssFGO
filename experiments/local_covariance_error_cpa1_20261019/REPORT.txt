1. Answer
---------
The new data do NOT support a universal CBS-ON covariance-collapse explanation.
GLIM's matched LOCAL covariance is almost unchanged between OFF and ON. Kimera
translation covariance decreases on SPX-3, but increases on EIG-2 and ETH1.
Kimera's relative translation error improves strongly on SPX-3 and worsens on
the other two. These are identical-endpoint comparisons, not trajectory ATE.

There IS a calibration concern: reported uncertainty is often much smaller
than the observed relative discrepancy against IE-TC, especially for rotation.
However, its magnitude depends on source, sequence, direction and revision age.
Covariance does not consistently rank GLIM's high-error versus low-error edges
on EIG-2 or ETH1. Kimera translation has a more useful, but imperfect, ranking.
A scalar can change overall strength; it cannot repair an uninformative ranking,
mean bias, omitted correlation or a measurement/covariance convention mismatch.

This audit is NOT a recommended numeric scale, proof of true NEES calibration,
or attribution of the fusion loss to a particular factor family. Nothing was
changed or rerun in any estimator. See the bounded next gate in section 10.

3. Method and units
-------------------
Reuse verified FL4z read_events/measure/summarize/paired_summary and FL4y
Reference/SE3 Exp/Log helpers, without editing them. Match OFF/ON by source and
physical (from_ns,to_ns); also assert matching source indices and source update.
Normalize GLIM's first-export timestamp-valued source_update to its state index.
Never match by transport revision number or pool revisions as independent data.

 Zref = inverse(Tref(t_a)) Tref(t_b)
 e = Log(inverse(Z) Zref) = [phi,rho]
 q6 = e^T Sigma^-1 e
 qphi = phi^T Sigma(phi,phi)^-1 phi
 qrho = rho^T Sigma(rho,rho)^-1 rho

These are the existing right-local CPT7-body coordinates. No global trajectory
alignment, scale fit or prism lever arm is applied to this body-relative test.
rho is SE3 tangent translation, not a newly introduced Cartesian residual.
Cartesian relative translation error is also retained separately in the JSON.
Full6x6 cross terms are retained for q6; block tests use marginal3x3 matrices,
not conditional Schur complements. Rotation is reported in degrees, rho in mm.

U = sqrt(trace(Sigma_block)) is a norm uncertainty scale. It is NOT a per-axis
standard deviation or a95%radius. Tables distinguish median U from RMS error.
ON/OFF covariance ratios are medians of paired trace ratios, not ratios of
pooled medians. Trace is not the graph's weight: directional Jacobians,
Sigma^-1, connectivity, WNOA and the marginal prior matter in optimization.

Four stored stages:
 - first: one first-publication record per matching edge;
 - last_common_update: latest source update present in BOTH modes for that edge;
 - age4s_first and age4s_later: SAME subset before/after exactly+40GLIM or
   +20Kimera source updates, approximately4s. This is source-update age, not
   receipt delay or an exactly4s wall-clock condition.
Latest-common is capture-censored and not equally mature across all edges.
Missing exact update-age records are excluded explicitly, not interpolated.

9. Limits: what this does NOT establish
--------------------------------------
IE-TC shares CPT7 with both sources. Its uncertainty and cross-correlation with
source errors are unknown here. These q values are normalized reference
discrepancies, NOT innovation NIS or true NEES against independent noiseless GT.
Under a simple common-chart approximation, discrepancy covariance would include
source covariance + reference covariance - cross terms. We have not estimated
the latter terms; cannot assign all excess discrepancy to the sender alone.
An empirical ellipsoid fraction is therefore descriptive, not a formal95%
coverage rejection or a numeric calibration certificate.

Edges overlap; revisions share data; adjacent errors are correlated. Thousands
of versions are not thousands of independent trials. Three short sequence
windows cannot establish a universal scale. Nor does a small positive SPD
matrix establish correctness of the model that produced it.

LOCAL removes explicitly inherited peer precision as defined/tested in CP-D1;
it does not remove all common information from corrected MAIN means, charts,
correspondences or shared CPT7. This audit does not repeat that provenance proof.

Raw source intervals here are NOT the receiver-aligned peer intervals after
temporal mean alignment. This audit does not certify those injection covariances
or measure the actual GLIM/Kimera/WNOA/marginal-prior Hessian competition.
Native trajectory ATE improvements need not imply better relative beliefs.
ETH1's improved native Kimera ATE therefore does not contradict its worse local
rho error. No revision reconstruction is substituted for original trajectories.
