BOTTOM LINE
===========
The saved first GLIM factor is fitted through a coupled pose/velocity/bias change,
not just a harmless rigid reorientation of Kimera's window. Keeping that same
pose/velocity correction but omitting its gyro-bias correction gives a very large
IMU residual. That is a cost probe, NOT a frozen-bias optimized solution.

The existing identical-graph no-CBS control (CP52, not rerun) shows the tradeoff:
Kimera fits the external factor while its native visual, IMU and bias-evolution
fit become worse. This supports a mechanism for the first disturbance, not proof
of a single root cause of all later trajectory zigzags or true bias error.

approximate Jacobians. Three factor/phase derivative checks fail the predeclared
1e-6 normalized gate reproducibly at BOTH perturbation spacings. Independent
exact SE3 cost/derivative reconstruction confirms these discrepancies. Their
effect on the optimized response has NOT been tested here. Do not announce
"the bug is fixed", "all checks passed", or assume that a small directional
difference is harmless in the coupled system. No graph was optimized in CP63.
