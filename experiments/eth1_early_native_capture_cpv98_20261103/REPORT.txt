CONCLUSION / NEXT DECISION
There is direct evidence for state-dependent visual outlier/triangulation gating
changing native information before any marginalization. There is no demonstrated
matrix-transformation/inversion/scaling error in this test. More accepted camera
constraints can legitimately reduce covariance; they can also give conditional
confidence that does not capture state-selection/feedback uncertainty.
We have not established calibration or the causal effect on full-run GLIM ATE.
Before changing weights or the outlier threshold, the next useful saved-data
check is whether the newly accepted tracks and returned low-covariance beliefs
are genuinely consistent, distinguishing improved geometry from overconfidence.
Any IE-TC residual check is a reference comparison, not independent prism truth.
