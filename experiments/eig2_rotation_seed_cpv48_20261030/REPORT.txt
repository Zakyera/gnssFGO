CP-V48 -- new-pose rotation initialization test
2026-10-30. COMPLETE. Candidate rejected as a fix for the captured large tilts.

BOTTOM LINE
Correcting the new orientation's bias reference makes its initial IMU rotation
residual essentially zero, but does NOT remove the large subsequent window
rotations. After the ordinary solve plus configured refresh, all five net tilt
magnitudes change by less than0.0035deg. The worst cases remain about9.5deg.
This does not justify deploying this change or spending a full sequence on it.

The test supports rejecting the backend rotation seed as the dominant local
cause in these checkpoints. It does NOT prove that frontend rotation-guided
matching, cumulative bias history or every other initialization choice is sound.
No live fix, improved trajectory accuracy or complete root cause is claimed.

1. EXACT COMPARISON
Five independent saved CP-V44 CBS-ON windows21--25. For every window:
A: original initial state and native graph.
B: replace ONLY the new pose's rotation with the IMU prediction using the
   previous revised NavState and its latest bias. Preserve new translation,
   velocity/bias, all older states and every native graph factor/observation.

Each arm receives exactly one ordinary native GN step and one native refresh.
No new source replay, measurements, iterations-to-convergence, covariance work,
weight changes, CBS-Jacobian correction, frontend rematching or production patch.
Twenty experimental GN steps total, plus four synthetic fixture steps. Windows
are independent: B at22 starts from original saved history, not B at21.

5. LIMITS / DECISION
This result is useful negative evidence: the inconsistency identified in CP-V47
is real, but fixing just this backend rotation seed does not fix the observed
large rotations. Do not promote it or present it as the CBS-ON bug repair.

The test intentionally leaves original ON-conditioned history and already-made
frontend selections in place. It does not rule out a cumulative frontend effect
from earlier biased rotation guidance. It also does not test changing stereo
translation or reintegrating raw IMU samples; those are different hypotheses.
Complete snapshots still begin at21, whereas first OFF/ON divergence was6.
CP-V44 differs from CP-V42 at6; neither is relabelled an exact replay of the other.

No full rerun or additional experiment was launched. Any further diagnosis
should target evidence around the first CBS admission, not assume that this
rotation-seed change is promising because its initial residual decreased.
