Decision in brief
-----------------
Do not tune a covariance multiplier from the first-to-later shrinkage table.
The actual GLIM stream changes covariance provenance: ordinary first
publication uses the shared-native LOCAL Gaussian history; later diagnostic
revisions use MAIN joint marginals. gnssFGO consumes both without alteration.
This split was explicitly preserved in the S4d provider implementation, not
introduced by this audit and not proven to be a new regression. It must be
made explicit before calling later covariance a refinement of native-only
uncertainty. Stable-ID replacement itself is not the problem identified here.

Separately, the native-only FIRST covariance already fails to distinguish
important EIG-2 relative-error differences. The retained diagnostics support
local-model precision, not a calibrated bound on real motion error. No Schur
arithmetic failure, jitter, missing endpoint cross terms, or catastrophic
cancellation was found in the logged checks. The precise unmodelled physical
error source is not identified by these logs. Do not assert that the prior,
GPU cache, IMU, or correspondence model uniquely causes it.

6. What the first covariance measures, and what is not established
----------------------------------------------------------------
It is inverse local Gaussian information marginalized to the relative pose,
conditional on selected factors, geometry/correspondences, fixed history and
their assumed noise. It is not an empirical error estimator that automatically
inflates when GLIM disagrees with the physical reference.

For fixed Gaussian equations H, eta, covariance comes from H, while the mean
depends on eta. Changing the RHS can change the solution without changing
covariance. The provider does not estimate a new error variance from GT or
inflate C in proportion to a bad relative error. Nonlinear changes can alter H,
but that does not guarantee calibrated error tracking.

Native composition includes native IMU/bias constraints, not just LiDAR:
W/src/glim/src/glim/odometry/odometry_estimation_imu.cpp:281-284,339-353.
LiDAR graph creation can use fixed-target unary map factors when keyframes
leave the active lag (odometry_estimation_gpu.cpp:209-221,249-260).
Those map poses are conditioned constants for that factor, not extra uncertain
graph states. These are model-scope facts, NOT isolated causes of EIG-2 error.

The logs do not retain full native H, endpoint P blocks, factor-family
information breakdown, or paired native/MAIN revised covariance snapshots for
these captures. Therefore exact attribution to LiDAR geometry, native IMU,
fixed-map treatment, accumulated prior, bias or reference error is unavailable.
No attempt to manufacture those quantities or launch a new diagnostic was made.

Conclusion: the first-stream confidence blind spot is real relative to the
retained reference; the physical error mechanism remains unisolated. The
downstream provenance split is established by code and payloads, but is NOT
proved to cause the fusion ATE difference. Both must be distinguished from a
Schur-complement arithmetic bug.
