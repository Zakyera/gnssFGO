CP-V92 — ETH1 split-IMU covariance audit
2026-11-02 | SAVED-DATA ANALYSIS COMPLETE; NO ESTIMATOR OR SETTINGS CHANGES

CONCLUSION
There is evidence of excessive confidence relative to IE-TC discrepancies in
Kimera's revised relative beliefs, especially as they mature. This is NOT a
universal CBS-ON covariance collapse: actual first-transmitted GLIM covariances
are almost unchanged; first-transmitted Kimera translation covariance does not
show a general decrease. Rotation is a separate, already under-calibrated-looking
problem in both modes. Unknown reference uncertainty/correlation prevents an
exact claim about how much the TRUE estimator covariance is underestimated.
No production multiplier, fault attribution or algorithm repair is justified
from these diagnostics alone.

CP89 full ETH1 OFF vs CP91 attempt1 full ETH1 ON, GLIM Hesai+CPT7/HG4930 and
Kimera Alphasense stereo+Alpha IMU. Both persistent in ON, no fusion. Same

METHOD
Match OFF/ON by physical from/to timestamps, not source indices (one keyframe
differs), pooled versions or transport revision numbers. Compare right-local
6x6 covariances in CPT7 body coordinates. Kimera native Alpha means/covariances
are conjugated/adjoint-transformed with the author's T_alpha_cpt7. Actual wire
payloads match saved native versions within CP91 frame-audit tolerances.
Zref = inv(T_IE(a))*T_IE(b). Residual e=Log(inv(Z_est)*Zref)=[phi,rho].
No global alignment, fitted scale or prism lever arm enters this relative test.
Full q=e' inv(C) e and marginal block tests preserve dense cross terms.
EVO1.36.3 computes Euclidean translation/rotation errors at each exact belief
interval (~0.2s), not the separate trajectory delta0.5m RPE statistic. Tangent
rho is used for covariance whitening; its RMS differs from the EVO Cartesian
translation RMS by <0.000005mm in the reported cohorts.
sqrt(trace(Cblock)) is a norm uncertainty, NOT per-axis sigma or a95%bound.
Pooled RMS norm uncertainty is sqrt(mean(trace(Cblock))). Variance changes below
compare mean trace with mean squared residual on precisely the same population.
Paired median trace ratios are explicitly labeled and are a different statistic.

6. INTERPRETATION AND NEXT PROPOSED CHECK
Evidence supports investigating the revised Kimera covariance's calibration and
history conditioning. It does NOT establish permanent beliefs as the unique
cause. More constraints normally shrink Hessian-based covariance; model bias,
unmodeled correlations and reference errors can prevent matching accuracy gains.
Persistent marginal priors carry peer history in this actual configuration;
filtering visible peer factors cannot establish native-only information. Different
IMUs remove one shared raw input, not persistent feedback correlation.
Corrected MAIN means plus LOCAL covariance are also a declared approximation,
not an exact posterior covariance for that corrected estimate.
Next proposed saved-data check: localize the2.2--4.2s covariance drop on a few
identified Kimera edges against marginalization/peer-prior absorption and exact
covariance-provider provenance. If necessary evidence is absent, request a
bounded instrumented test; do not infer the missing Hessians or change weights.
No next capture or tuning is authorized or launched by this report.

LIMITATIONS
IE-TC shares CPT7 with GLIM; ON Kimera can inherit correlated information.
Unknown reference covariance and cross-correlation mean discrepancy covariance
is not simply sender covariance. e' inv(C) e is normalized reference discrepancy,
NOT NIS or true NEES against independent noiseless ground truth. The much larger
rotational discrepancy could include reference/model/timing error. We cannot
attribute its entire magnitude to an incorrect estimator covariance.
Source first/revised beliefs are distinct from receiver-time-aligned injected
factors. Existing mean-only temporal covariance approximation is not repaired or
qualified here. Absolute trajectory ATE is not the error of a relative belief.
AP20 has no orientation and is not silently used as6DoF truth. No global fit or
independence certification. Numerical SPD is necessary but not calibration.
