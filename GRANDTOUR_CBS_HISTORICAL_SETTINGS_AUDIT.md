# GrandTour CBS historical settings and semantics audit

> **Correction following the Newer College audit, 25 September:** descriptions below that call receiver-edge competition/replacement a historical-to-current change are not established by the dirty source diff alone. The package HEADs date to August 22; the retained August 24 Newer College `cbs_receiver_edge_dedupe` report and log already document BPSAM alternative replacement. The cross-update Kimera match rule's exact introduction date remains unverified. Explicit September live revision is confirmed as a different mode, but classic Newer College rolling windows also repeated/reapplied beliefs without that protocol. See [the recovery audit](CLASSIC_CBS_BASELINE_RECOVERY_AUDIT.md) before selecting any rollback. Do not remove older replacement behavior on the basis of the table below.

**Scope (25 September 2026):** read-only examination of retained GrandTour runs and the current/committed CBS code. No estimator, fusion, covariance-calibration, or new sequence run was performed. ATE numbers below are *retained evaluations*, not recomputed results. `ON` and `OFF` are comparisons within each mission/window; a 40 s ATE must not be compared numerically with a 305 s ATE as though it were the same evaluation interval.

## Factor lifetime and code-history evidence

The pre-revision committed GLIM bridge (current `glim_ros1` HEAD `8ce5c09`, **not proven to be the exact September 21 binary**) has `shouldInject()` inserting a source/receiver edge into `injected_edges_` once, and `appendExternalFactor()` appending a `BetweenFactor<Pose3>` to `new_factors` without one-shot removal (`git show HEAD:src/glim_ros/cbs_bridge.cpp`, around lines 1792 and 2399). The historical EIG-2 `estimators.log` confirms all 1519 K→G `injected_persistent` rows have distinct source edges. GLIM's normal graph, not a CBS sidecar, received them.

The old Kimera launch specified `--cbs_odom_factor_mode=persistent`, local smoother covariance, and `--cbs_use_persistent_bpsam_for_main_backend=false`. Its BPSAM persistent branch tracks an active factor slot and removes/replaces the *same odometry key* when re-added; it is not a one-solve impulse (`git show HEAD:src/bpsam/bpsam.cpp`, around lines 4018–4040). The historical EIG-2 log confirms 1517 distinct G→K `sender_odom_queued` source edges. Once fixed-lag marginalization retires an endpoint, its effect can be carried by the resulting marginal prior; neither the old nor new saved artifacts contain a per-edge proof of how much of that influence survived. `setMarginalizationGraph(LOCAL)` excluding CBS factors is a **covariance-export/reference-graph** operation, not evidence that the main optimizer discarded the persistent factor immediately.

Current GLIM opt-in revision code is in [`cbs_bridge.cpp`](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/src/glim_ros1/src/glim_ros/cbs_bridge.cpp): publish current-snapshot revisions (around 1480–1565), coalesce pending versions (1248–1260, 1863–1883, 1976–1990), accept only newer versions (2010–2026), and remove the tracked older factor before insertion (2667–2697). Current Kimera [`VioBackend.cpp`](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/src/Kimera-VIO/src/backend/VioBackend.cpp) coalesces pending/retry versions (2121–2210), gates accepted revisions (2667–2678), and chooses/reuses receiver-edge matches (2920–3026). Current [`bpsam.cpp`](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/src/cbs/src/bpsam/bpsam.cpp) removes tracked persistent alternatives before inserting a replacement (around 4068–4092), then prunes tracking when slots disappear (3091 onward). The 40 s [revision audit](EIG2_REVISED_CBS_INFORMATION_COMPETITION_SHORT.md) records the actual thousands of accepted replacements and exact source-to-factor covariance transport. Replacement prevents stacking multiple *currently active* copies of the same edge; it does **not** make successive versions statistically independent, nor establish perfect removal of information already absorbed by a marginal prior.

`git diff` against the committed package HEADs additionally shows a Kimera **cross-update best timestamp-match rule** and BPSAM pending/active **alternate-receiver-edge replacement**, not just revision counters. These may affect pair selection and factor topology even with unchanged source covariances. There are also audit/logging additions and an opt-in Kimera IMU sample-support implementation; the latter defaults to the old `current` path and is not selected by the saved new runner. The GLIM covariance construction path and Kimera `schur_relative_between` configuration were not changed to a new source-weighting formula in the inspected diff. Because old run artifacts did not save per-package source SHAs or binaries, a complete commit-by-commit attribution between September 21 and 25 is **not possible**; current dirty working-tree diffs are evidence of present differences, not a definitive timestamped reconstruction.

Thus the historical full-run **translation** medians did put GLIM tighter than Kimera, particularly ETH1/SPX-3; SNOW-3's traces were much closer, and this was not universally true in rotation (SPX-3 is the counterexample). On the comparable early EIG-2 segment, old applied beliefs and new revision-0 source beliefs are broadly similar in scale. The apparent Kimera-tighter-translation claim comes primarily from **pooling many late Kimera revisions**: in the new audit Kimera's per-ID median translation trace falls from `2.803e-6` at first publication to `2.213e-7` at its last; GLIM falls from `2.083e-6` to `9.800e-7`. That is a different belief age and feedback regime, not evidence of a newly introduced Kimera covariance multiplier. The new transport check found max source→factor matrix difference `0.0` over the logged 40 s path. We have **not** proven that those small covariances are statistically calibrated or that revision replacement is safe after marginalization.

## Direct answers and next controlled test

1. **Best old known-good CBS run:** the retained full-305-s EIG-2 bidirectional ON run, with its matched OFF control. ETH1 and SPX-3 independently corroborate the historical strong-Kimera-improvement behavior (with ETH1 playback-rate limitations). **SNOW-3 does not:** the same old semantic family barely improves Kimera and worsens GLIM.
2. **Same estimator/CBS settings?** Same saved EIG-2 sensor profiles, GPU GLIM selection, Kimera lag setting, IMU topic, horizon/tolerances, first-belief covariance formula, scale `1.0`, disabled health weighting, and persistent mode. **Not the same effective CBS experiment:** 40 versus 305 s, live multi-version publication/replacement versus observed one accepted version per edge, and additional receiver-edge selection/replacement logic.
3. **What changed besides revision handling?** The run window and audit/presentation settings, plus Kimera's cross-update best receiver-edge match and BPSAM's alternate pending/active receiver-edge replacement in the current working code. The latter are genuine CBS behavioral changes, not merely logging. No documented source-profile, Q/IMU gflag, covariance scale/floor/health-setting change explains the result. Exact historic source commits remain unpinned.
4. **Historical covariance/weighting explanation?** Old full runs usually gave GLIM the tighter translational belief; old early EIG-2 and new revision-0 medians are similar. Later Kimera revisions become markedly tighter, reversing the pooled translation comparison. That changing covariance and feedback is a plausible *mechanism to test*, not a proven causal explanation for the 40 s ATE degradation. No retained evidence shows a scale/floor switch.
5. **Smallest next experiment (not executed):** on the **same EIG-2 40 s window**, retain identical saved profiles, code build, bags, playback, factor mode and receiver selection, and change **only** `CBSMS_CBS_LIVE_REVISION_ENABLE=true` to `false`, with a matched OFF control if not already source-equivalent. Log first-belief IDs/covariances, accepted factor slots and retirements, and score both native estimators on the same 40 s reference. A small additional code-provenance snapshot (per-package SHA plus dirty diff hash) must accompany that future run. This isolates the broad revision regime from window difficulty before any weighting study; it would not isolate the independent cross-update receiver-match code change without a further specifically authorized control.

**Conclusion:** the new 40 s CBS-ON run is **not genuinely equivalent** to the historical successful CBS-ON runs. The largest verified difference is repeated stable-ID revision replacement and its later, often much tighter belief covariances; a changed Kimera receiver-edge competition rule is also present. The new run alone cannot establish that historical CBS was misweighted or that revision replacement caused the ATE change. No new run or code change was made in this audit.
