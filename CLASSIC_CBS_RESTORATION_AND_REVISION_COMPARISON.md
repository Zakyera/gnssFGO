# Classic CBS restoration and explicit live-revision comparison

Date: 2026-09-25. Scope: native GLIM/Kimera CBS; no EKF or gnssFGO fusion inference. This report distinguishes **observed historical execution**, **current source-path behavior**, and **unproven source-binary chronology**. A recovered ATE alone does not prove an exact binary reproduction.

| CBS mechanism | Historical classic: retained evidence | Current opt-in live revision |
|---|---|---|
| Relative mean source | GLIM: endpoint `between` from its current odometry smoother. Kimera: endpoint `between` from its local fixed-lag smoother. A rolling window could regenerate an active edge on a later update. | Same first-publication source, then `between` recomputed for every still-active tracked edge at successive smoother snapshots. |
| Covariance source | `schur_relative_between`, dense 6×6 joint-endpoint relative covariance. GLIM GrandTour used the direct odometry smoother. Kimera called `setMarginalizationGraph(LOCAL)` before `getOdometryBeliefs`, excluding tracked explicit CBS factors and anchors from the local marginal graph, but not proving exclusion of CBS information already absorbed into a fixed-lag prior. | Same formula and nominal graph selection. Later revisions use later snapshots, and their covariance can tighten markedly. |
| Republish trigger | Sender's ordinary `time_horizon_window` publication on backend/smoother update; up to the configured most-recent pair count. Newer College's wider per-update budget repeatedly sent active edges; GrandTour's one-pair budget yielded about one **accepted** source edge/frame in the retained full runs. Accepted-ID uniqueness does not prove no repeated transmission. | In addition to the ordinary publication, active tracked edges are reevaluated after updates and republished with increased revision when the mean or covariance changes above `1e-12`. |
| Edge identity | Source agent + sender `(from,to)` indices in payload; receiver matches physical endpoint timestamps to its own poses. GLIM classic deduplicates a five-tuple of source/receiver indices. Kimera's BPSAM persistent slot also identifies the receiver odometry edge and source identity. | Explicit stable source BeliefID `(agent,from,to)` plus monotone revision. |
| Duplicate / pending rule | GLIM rejects an already injected five-tuple. Kimera selects the best candidate per receiver edge within one backend update; BPSAM replaces pending same-receiver or same-sender alternatives instead of stacking them. The introduction date of the **current** cross-update best-match gate is not pinned by a historical binary. | Newest revision per stable source ID is coalesced in incoming/deferred queues; stale/equal revisions are ignored. A newer same-source revision can supersede a previous candidate. |
| Replacement rule | Historical Newer College BPSAM persistent factors were reapplied/replaced under same-sender and alternative-sender odometry-key rules. Four 45 s repeats recorded 281–378 same-sender reapplications and 6–12 alternatives; median 6.5–8 applications/receiver edge, maximum 17–19. This is **not** a no-revision/no-replacement algorithm. | Tracked old active factor is removed and replaced by the newer version in one graph update. If the old slot has retired, the log reports `old_factor_retired`; removal of its marginalized information is not established. |
| Factor lifetime | Persistent, not a one-solve impulse; active while endpoint keys remain and until replacement or fixed-lag retirement. The resulting marginal prior can retain prior information. | Same fixed-lag limitation, with many more opportunities for active-slot replacement before retirement. |
| Marginalization behavior | Kimera local covariance graph removes tracked explicit CBS factors; native optimization still uses them. Neither historical run files nor current code prove that CBS information is removable after it enters the marginal prior. | Frequent revisions do not solve prior-history removal; an old active factor may be replaced, but a retired factor's prior contribution is not undone. |

## Historical evidence and covariance path

- Successful native Kimera OFF→ON ATE (m): Newer College Quad-Easy `6.069798→2.465788`; GrandTour ETH1 `9.114752→0.662930`, SPX-3 `1.056540→0.243733`, EIG-2 `1.309657→0.479174`. These use different dataset-specific camera, IMU, lag, playback and receive-delay settings. The [recovery audit](CLASSIC_CBS_BASELINE_RECOVERY_AUDIT.md) records provenance and counterexamples.
- Historical SPX-3 launch/resolved parameters specify bidirectional persistent factors, `time_horizon_window` around 0.20 s, one pair/update, `schur_relative_between`, factor covariance scale 1.0, and health weighting disabled. The saved runner and launch differ from current copies principally by added live-revision and audit arguments; they do **not** pin the historical GLIM/Kimera/CBS binary hashes.
- Source generation and covariance: [`GlimCbsBridge::publishOutgoingBeliefs`](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/src/glim_ros1/src/glim_ros/cbs_bridge.cpp) selects pairs, gets endpoint means and joint marginals from the direct odometry smoother; that direct smoother is also where received K→G persistent factors are inserted, so its joint marginal can contain CBS feedback. [`BPSAM::getOdometryBeliefs`](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/src/cbs/src/bpsam/bpsam.cpp) computes `between`, joint information and the full relative covariance for Kimera after `LOCAL` graph selection. That local selection drops tracked explicit CBS factors but does not establish removal of their historical marginal-prior information. The current opt-in revision paths use those same operations on later smoother snapshots.
- Transport: [`CbsRelativeFrameConversion::convertExternalToBase`](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/src/Kimera-VIO-ROS/src/CbsRelativeFrameConversion.cpp) applies the Pose3 adjoint to the full 6×6 covariance. Kimera's outbound ROS wrapper applies the inverse frame conversion when required. GrandTour's shared CPT7 frame makes this identity in the selected SPX-3 profile; Newer College needs the nontrivial calibrated transform.
- The retained Newer College [accepted-factor covariance audit](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/runs/newer_college_maths_easy_cbs_on_four_repeat_20260824/a/inserted_factor_covariances/REPORT.md) reconstructs every accepted full 6×6 matrix from the belief bag and production frame transform, with 339 G→K and 79 K→G factors, all SPD, and maximum receiver-log cross-check relative error `3.758e-6`. The GrandTour SPX-3 historical directory has receiver covariance rows but **no saved belief bag or binary**, so bytewise source→receiver matrix identity cannot be retroactively certified for that run.
- Historical Newer College [replacement audit](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/runs/newer_college_maths_easy_cbs_on_four_repeat_20260824/persistent_replacement_comparison/REPORT.md) proves that same-sender reapplication predates the explicit September BeliefID/revision protocol. The [August 24 receiver-edge diagnostic](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/runs/newer_college_maths_easy_cbs_receiver_edge_dedupe_20260824/REPORT.md) documents alternative replacement before the successful September GrandTour runs.

## Named protocol selector

The existing GrandTour runner now accepts opt-in `CBSMS_CBS_PROTOCOL=classic|live_revision`; `classic` selects the already-existing `cbs_live_revision_enable=false` path and `live_revision` selects `true`. If unset, the previous `CBSMS_CBS_LIVE_REVISION_ENABLE` behavior is preserved. A conflicting explicit old/new selector is rejected. The selected name is stored in run metadata. This is a **runner-level selector**, not a new CBS algorithm or a claim that the current `false` path is byte-identical to the unpinned historical binary. The conditional receiver-match code in the current Kimera checkout remains an unresolved chronology question; it has not been removed or changed.

`bash -n` passes and an invalid protocol exits with code 2 before starting ROS. No GLIM, Kimera, BPSAM, covariance, weighting, or optimizer source was changed by this selector.

## Reproduction / controlled comparison

The selected known-good sequence is GrandTour SPX-3, mission `2024-11-02-17-43-10`, bag offset 32.0 s, duration 134.0 s, playback 0.30×, stereo front cameras + shared CPT7 IMU, 25-state Kimera lag. Exact retained GLIM and Kimera profile trees were copied and compare byte-for-byte with the saved successful ON configuration. Both new controls disable only the presentation sidecar/visualizer; they retain identical native sensor, CBS, source and scoring settings. The first presentation-enabled preflight was stopped because a stale *optional EKF sidecar* expects an older ROS belief message MD5. It is not an estimator result.

Both matched native-only runs completed with 1,332 GLIM and 667 published Kimera poses. They were scored separately with the unchanged `score_grandtour_prism.py` using the same SPX-3 prism bag, SE(3) Kabsch/no-scale alignment, CPT7-to-prism lever arm, and valid-GT interpolation gate. The runner's presentation-off branch omits scoring, so the exact existing scorer was invoked after each completed run; no new metric implementation was introduced.

The ON run metadata records 1,330 outgoing G→K belief messages, 659 accepted G→K receiver edges, and 660 accepted K→G receiver edges. These are message/event counts, not the number of simultaneously active factors.

The **matched** OFF→ON Kimera improvement is `0.422881 m` (63.24%). New ON is within `0.002081 m` (0.85%) of the archived ON ATE, while GLIM remains centimetric. The new OFF Kimera result is materially better than the archived OFF despite byte-identical copied GLIM/Kimera profile trees; the archived OFF must not be substituted as the control for the new ON. Presentation load, asynchronous execution and unpinned source binaries are possible contributors; this audit has not attributed the OFF difference to one mechanism. The new comparison covers 550 Kimera poses over 108.999 s of GT overlap in both modes.

The accepted **G→K ordered source/receiver edge sequence is identical** to archived SPX-3 (`659/659` rows). The K→G **source-edge sequence is also identical** (`660/660`), but **179/660 receiver endpoint bindings differ**. The first mismatch is Kimera `k3→k4`: archived GLIM target `g1→g3` has endpoint gaps 15.95/16.09 ms, whereas the new run bound `g0→g2` with gaps 83.76/84.06 ms. The accepted-factor log time differs as well (old simulated clock ≈`1730565903.998`, new ≈`1730565905.277`). The GLIM and Kimera output timestamp sequences at the start are identical; the difference is in receiver association/availability, not an identified change in physical sensor stamps. We cannot attribute it uniquely to scheduling, matching code, or factor-graph state from these logs. This fails **exact factor-map reproduction** despite equal counts and near-equal ON ATE.

The new ON run's [full-matrix covariance audit](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/runs/classic_cbs_restoration_20260925/spx3_classic_on_native/covariance_audit/REPORT.md) associated all 659 G→K and 660 K→G accepted factors with the lossless recorded belief bag. All 1,319 matrices were SPD, the configured scale was 1.0, health inflation was off, and the maximum receiver-log cross-check relative error was `4.747e-6` (G→K zero). Median rotation/translation covariance-block traces were `1.106e-7 rad² / 2.726e-6 m²` for G→K and `3.848e-8 rad² / 2.088e-5 m²` for K→G. These are near the archived applied-factor medians (`8.075e-8 / 2.480e-6` and `3.771e-8 / 2.161e-5`, respectively), but not byte-identical source covariances. No `receiver_edge_retained_better_persistent_match`, pending-alternative replacement or active-alternative replacement row appeared in the new SPX-3 ON log; each accepted source/receiver edge was unique. Thus the uncertain current cross-update gate did not visibly reject an edge in this run, but its historical availability remains unproven.

The final active Kimera CBS-factor count is **not recorded** by the presentation-off run path. This requested lifecycle diagnostic remains open; inventing a count would overstate certification.

## Current limits on certification

The saved September GrandTour runs contain copied launch/config and native trajectories but do not pin package source diffs or executable hashes. The current nested package HEADs date to August 22 and have accumulated dirty changes. In particular, the present Kimera cross-update best persistent receiver-edge match gate is in the dirty source diff; its presence in the successful September binary cannot be established from the retained artifacts. The historical GrandTour logs also do not contain per-edge full sender matrices for an exact transport equality proof. The new run establishes the **beneficial native behavior, accepted counts, exact G→K map and current full-matrix transport**. It does **not** establish exact old/current K→G receiver matching or the final active factor count. Therefore the named `classic` selector is an operational **candidate**, not a certified byte-for-byte or event-for-event historical mode. Do not use ATE alone as an equivalence certificate.

### Direct answers and conditional stop

1. **Have we faithfully recovered the historical mechanism?** Partially, not fully certified. The retained and current classic paths agree on published native product, G→K and K→G accepted counts, G→K edge map, scale/health configuration, full dense covariance formula, and material Kimera benefit. They do not reproduce 179 K→G receiver bindings, and the archived source binary/lifecycle state is unavailable. The exact rule provenance therefore remains unresolved.
2. **Does the candidate classic mode materially improve weak Kimera?** Yes, in the matched SPX-3 native controls: `0.668695→0.245814 m` ATE; GLIM remains below 2 cm ATE. This is an observed current result, not proof that every historical event was replayed.
3. **What does explicit live revision change?** Stable `(agent,from,to)` BeliefID, numerical revision, active-window republishing of later smoother means/covariances, newest-per-ID queue coalescing, stale-version rejection, and tracked active-factor replacement. Historical Newer College already had unnumbered rolling-window reapplications/replacements; the new protocol changes their trigger, identity and frequency, not merely whether replacement exists at all.
4. **Smallest candidate explanation for recent degradation?** The opt-in protocol exposes receivers to far more successive versions, whose Kimera covariance became much tighter in the existing EIG-2 40 s audit (per-ID median translation trace `2.803e-6→2.213e-7 m²` from first to last). Repeated nonlinear replacement and possible prior-history feedback are testable mechanisms, **not causal findings**. The present SPX-3 classic run did not test them.

Because the user required the classic mechanism to be **certified first**, the live-revision comparison was **not run** after discovering the K→G association discrepancy and missing binary/lifetime provenance. No fusion, tuning, covariance calibration, CI, health weighting, or topology change was made. The previously stopped presentation-enabled preflight (stale optional EKF message MD5) is excluded from all tables.
