# SPX-3 CBS-OFF GLIM-only revision-preservation test

Date: 2026-09-26. This is a source-representation control, **not** a Kimera, CBS-ON, GNSS, weighting, or Qc-tuning experiment. The receiver remains full-history iSAM2 with the same Combined IMU, WNOJ, four initial priors, 59-sample initialization, GLIM support grid, ALL_RIGHT CPT7 integration, and unscaled dense GLIM covariances used in [the preceding model audit](GNSSFGO_SPX3_GLIM_ONLY_MODEL_TUNING_AUDIT.md).

## Result and decision

**Revisions remove the dominant SPX-3 final-graph GLIM-preservation loss.** On one controlled, freshly logged CBS-OFF source execution, first-publication R0 has 0.140504 m prism ATE; causal stable-ID replacement R1 has 0.010616 m, and the noncausal last-jointly-valid R2 has 0.010623 m. Same-run native GLIM is 0.007531 m. R1 closes **97.68%** of the native-to-R0 ATE gap and differs from R2 by **0.392 mm RMS** in raw, unaligned position. The independent pose-only graph makes the source-side mechanism especially clear: each parity chain falls from roughly 0.15–0.16 m ATE under first publications to roughly 0.0072 m under last-valid revisions.

The condition for a **working retrospectively revised GLIM-only gnssFGO baseline** is met. Freeze the revised GLIM belief representation with the current Combined IMU, WNOJ, priors, support grid, and timing for the next separately authorized study; there is no first-order reason here to tune Qc. This is **not** a claim that the instantaneous/as-played receiver matches native GLIM: its R1 online ATE is 0.036204 m, with 1 s t-RPE 0.026580 m and r-RPE 1.754214°. The 0.010616 m result is the final graph after later causally arriving revisions have retroactively improved earlier support states. The source's median last-valid revision arrives 4.821 s after its physical edge end. Any real-time claim must use the online product, not the retrospectively revised one.

## Source and population validity

The completed archived SPX-3 source bag had no full active-pose revision log (`glim_cbs_export_endpoint_audit_enable: false`), so later full mean-plus-covariance beliefs could not be reconstructed from it. The diagnostic rerun is [here](/home/yeranis/repos/V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/runs/_spx3_glim_revision_preservation_slow_20260926/). It used the saved [OFF GLIM configuration](/home/yeranis/repos/V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/runs/classic_cbs_restoration_20260925/spx3_off_native/glim_config), Hesai and CPT7 only, CBS OFF, observe-only outgoing beliefs, 5 s smoother lag, 0.20 s target horizon, one horizon pair per update, and `schur_relative_between` full 6×6 covariance. The [source launcher](tools/spx3_glim_revision_source.sh) enabled the pre-existing read-only diagnostic endpoint/revision log; `glim_cbs_live_revision_enable` remained false, and no revised factor was injected into GLIM. [The logger](../V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/src/glim_ros1/src/glim_ros/cbs_bridge.cpp) computes each later active-pose relative mean and a new joint-marginal-derived covariance after a smoother update. The resolved GLIM parameters differ from the archived source only in diagnostic logging, dump path, and unused inspector recording ID.

The final diagnostic playback used 31.9 s bag start, 134.1 s duration, and 0.10× playback. The extra 0.1 s pre-roll restores the archived first accepted support timestamp; the slower rate prevents the heavy diagnostic logging from starving GLIM. The selected **1,330 IDs** retain *exactly* the archived `(source, from_index, to_index, from_timestamp_ns, to_timestamp_ns)` grid: zero mismatches, from `0→2` through `1329→1331`. All scored full-graph products have 1,332 support states, 1,093 identical AP20-prism matches over 108.795 s, and 1,321 identical 1 s IE-TC RPE pairs. The fresh R0 payload is **not byte-identical** to the archived source execution: relative-mean coordinate RMS difference 0.000622 and full-covariance-entry RMS difference `1.321e-8`; fresh R0 differs from the archived receiver trajectory by 0.02123 m raw-position RMS. Its ATE 0.140504 m nevertheless closely reproduces the archived 0.142027 m. All causal comparisons below therefore use the *same fresh source run*, not a mixture of archived and freshly logged beliefs.

The [unchanged bag exporter](/home/yeranis/repos/V4RL/cbs_gtsam4.3/runs/_eig2_glim_endpoint_revision_audit_20260923/export_cbs_unique_relative_beliefs.py) provided revision 0 and its bag receipt time. The [validated revision converter](tools/eig2_revision_stream_export.py) matched **all 1,330** bag means and all 36 covariance entries to source revision-0 log rows within `1e-12`. For later versions it used the source log's availability time, recomputed full covariance, and stable ID `(g,from_index,to_index)`. This is an **offline diagnostic revision transport**, not a claim that the live CBS wire already publishes these versions. One floating-point timestamp inversion placed revision 1 of ID `1→3` **166 ns** before its bag-recorded revision 0. The SPX-3-only converter option clamps that one time to the first receipt; it rejects inversions above 1 µs. No mean, covariance, endpoint, or revision number was repaired. There were no other inversions, invalid covariances, duplicate selected first publications, or source revision errors. Independent validation of all 63,237 full matrices found zero nonfinite entries, zero symmetry error, and minimum eigenvalue `4.47e-9`.

Two preliminary source attempts were **excluded before graph scoring**: one had a one-frame startup-grid shift and its launcher did not complete cleanly; a 0.30× logged rerun fell behind and lost `i→i+2` topology. They are not evidence for or against revision preservation. Only the complete 0.10× source run supplies R0/R1/R2.

## Exact controls and metrics

R0 inserts each first publication once at its record availability time. R1 starts from the same first version, then removes the old factor and inserts each later complete mean-plus-covariance version of the *same* BeliefID at logged availability time. R2 inserts the last jointly covariance-valid version at its physical edge endpoint, knowingly noncausal. The full graph's IMU, WNOJ, prior, source-covariance, and support-state code paths are otherwise identical. No Kimera CSV/factor was loaded. R3 was **not run**: 47 of the 1,330 selected IDs remained active/right-censored at source shutdown, so a genuinely finalized mean for every ID is unavailable. R2 uses the last **observed jointly valid** version for those tail IDs and is not mislabeled as finalized.

The pose-only graph contains exactly the 1,330 native dense-covariance `i→i+2` factors and two tight initial-pose gauge priors, one per disconnected parity tree. Its optimized factor cost is below `2.4e-21` in every case. Per-parity ATE is reported separately because each chain has its own gauge; combining their two separately aligned errors into one nominal pose-only ATE would be misleading. R1 and R2 pose-only final factor sets and output files are byte-identical. Direct composition against same-run native positions drops from **0.34969 m RMS (R0)** to **0.00952 m RMS (last valid)**; orientation drops from 1.7442° to 0.0856° RMS. These trees fit all edges essentially exactly, so their trajectory change is driven by **revised means**, not covariance reweighting. The full IMU/WNOJ graph uses revised means *and* revised covariances together; this test does not separately attribute their full-graph effects. The large drift already exists in the first-publication source representation, before IMU or WNOJ.

## Revision and covariance diagnostics

The source provided **63,237** valid versions of 1,330 IDs: versions per ID median 48, mean 47.55, p95/max 49, minimum 3 at the right-censored tail. First-publication latency from physical endpoint is median/mean/p95/max **0.1169/0.1164/0.1202/0.3371 s**. Last-observed-valid latency is **4.8206/4.7810/4.9186/4.9311 s**. Forty-seven selected IDs had not retired by shutdown. R1 records **1,330 insertions and 61,907 replacements**, zero duplicate/stale versions, zero Kimera factors, and exactly 1,330 accepted GLIM IDs. Maximum absolute factor-versus-source covariance difference is `4.66e-21`. Receiver wall time was 13.82 s for R0, 188.88 s for R1 (173.70 s inside revision updates), and 12.32 s for R2; this is diagnostic cost, not an end-to-end live-throughput guarantee.

First→last relative-mean change across IDs is **1.340 mm median / 1.800 mm RMS** in translation and **0.0270° median / 0.0395° RMS** in rotation. Median full-source covariance-block traces change as follows, with every covariance recomputed rather than frozen or scaled:

| Block trace | Revision 0 median | Last valid median | Median per-ID last/first ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Rotation (rad²) | `1.3043e-7` | `1.0340e-7` | 0.7942 |
| Translation (m²) | `3.0696e-6` | `1.9013e-6` | 0.6186 |

R1 and R2 final trajectories have **0.000392 m RMS / 0.001460 m max** raw-position separation and **0.00767° RMS** raw-orientation separation over all 1,332 common timestamps. Their ATE differs by 7.4 µm. The small nonzero receiver difference is compatible with different incremental factor insertion/relinearization histories; the pose-only final factor-set identity shows no source-payload difference between their endpoints. This is not byte-equivalence of R1 and R2.
